# Acacia Sapling

## Obtaining

Acacia Saplings can drop from decaying Acacia Leaves. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Acacia Sapling is planted to grow acacia trees.

## Behavior

It grows over time when planted on valid soil with enough light. Bone Meal can be used to try to grow it immediately.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_sapling` block.
* It appears in the Natural Blocks creative tab.
