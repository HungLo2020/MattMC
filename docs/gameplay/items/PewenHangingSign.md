# Pewen Hanging Sign

## Obtaining

Pewen Hanging Sign can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Pewen Hanging Sign is used to display editable text in the world.

## Behavior

It keeps its written text after placement and can be dyed or waxed to control editing.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:pewen_hanging_sign`.
