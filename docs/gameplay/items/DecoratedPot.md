# Decorated Pot

## Obtaining

Decorated Pot can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Decorated Pot is a decorative pottery block that can display sherd patterns.

## Behavior

Decorated Pots can be crafted with pottery sherds and broken to recover their decorated form or components according to normal rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:decorated_pot` block.
