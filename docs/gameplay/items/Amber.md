# Amber

Amber is the inventory form of the transparent [Amber block](../blocks/Amber.md), integrated from Alex's Caves. Its ID is `minecraft:amber`.

## Obtaining and use

Choose Amber from the Creative inventory and place it for transparent decorative construction. Its light transmission, hardness, drops, and current Survival-acquisition limits are described on the block page.

No separate item-only activation is established for Amber. Its item registration uses the standard placeable block form. It should not be confused with [Ambersol](Ambersol.md), which produces its own light.
