# Petrified Oak Slab

**Petrified Oak Slab** is the item form of `minecraft:petrified_oak_slab`. The [block guide](../blocks/PetrifiedOakSlab.md#petrified-oak-slab) owns its placed behavior. [Item registration][item]

## Obtaining

**No ordinary fresh-Survival acquisition route was found in the checked bundle.** The item has no producing recipe or natural structure occurrence, and the only checked loot is recovery of an already placed slab. See [availability and legacy data](../blocks/PetrifiedOakSlab.md#survival-availability).

## Usage

Place an existing slab in the upper or lower half of a block space, or combine two matching Petrified Oak Slabs into a double slab. Ordinary Oak Slabs cannot combine with it.

## Behavior

An unbroken pickaxe is required to collect it: a single slab gives one item and a double gives two. It is not ordinary wooden-slab fuel. See [placement and water](../blocks/PetrifiedOakSlab.md#placement-doubling-and-water) and [material properties](../blocks/PetrifiedOakSlab.md#material-properties).
