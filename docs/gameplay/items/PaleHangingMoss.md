# Pale Hanging Moss

Pale Hanging Moss is the item for **`minecraft:pale_hanging_moss`**. Recover placed segments with **Shears or Silk Touch**; hand breaking and removing the ceiling do not recover them. Fortune adds no quantity. [Item][items] · [Loot][loot-pale_hanging_moss]

A selected Wandering Trader offer sells **3 for 1 Emerald**, with four uses. Natural Pale Garden tree decorations are another source. There is no crafting recipe. See [finding a starting supply](../blocks/MossAndPaleMoss.md#finding-a-starting-supply), including why player-grown Pale Oak is a different route. [Trade][trades]

Place it beneath a valid ceiling or another Pale Hanging Moss segment. Bone Meal on any segment extends the connected strand by one block if air is available below its tip. It does not grow automatically, hold water, or provide a climbing surface. [Block callbacks][hanging-moss]

Use the [Pale Hanging Moss guide](../blocks/MossAndPaleMoss.md#pale-hanging-moss) for support loss and collection, and [Hanging Roots](../blocks/HangingRootsAndSporeBlossom.md#hanging-roots) for the waterloggable plant with different loot rules.
