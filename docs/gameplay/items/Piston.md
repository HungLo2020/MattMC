# Piston

The Piston item places `minecraft:piston`, a block that pushes an eligible load when powered.

## Obtaining and use

Craft it from three accepted Planks, four Cobblestone, one Iron Ingot, and one Redstone Dust using the [shared Piston recipe](../blocks/Pistons.md#crafting-and-collecting). A placed Piston returns its item in ordinary Survival mining; a pickaxe is the tagged tool but is not required for the drop.

The head points opposite your nearest look direction at placement. The [Piston guide](../blocks/Pistons.md) explains power, the 12-block movement limit, immovable and destroyed blocks, and a small test layout. An ordinary Piston does not pull its pushed block back. Combine this item with a Slimeball to craft a [Sticky Piston](StickyPiston.md).
