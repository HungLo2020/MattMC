# Lime Glazed Terracotta

## Obtaining

Lime Glazed Terracotta can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Lime Glazed Terracotta is a patterned decorative block used in floors, walls, and ornamental builds.

## Behavior

Its pattern orientation depends on placement direction.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:lime_glazed_terracotta` block.
