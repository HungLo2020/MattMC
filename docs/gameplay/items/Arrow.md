# Arrow

## Obtaining

Arrows are crafted from Flint, Sticks, and Feathers. They can also be found as loot or obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Arrows are ammunition for bows, crossbows, dispensers, and other arrow-firing mechanics.

## Behavior

Arrows are consumed when fired in survival unless the weapon or mode prevents consumption. Fired arrows can damage entities and may be recoverable depending on how they were shot.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:arrow`.
* It appears in the Combat creative tab.
