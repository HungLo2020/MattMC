# Kangaroo Burger

## Obtaining

Kangaroo Burger can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Kangaroo Burger is a food item from bundled mob content.

## Behavior

Eating it restores hunger and saturation according to its configured food values.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:kangaroo_burger`.
* This item comes from bundled mod content integrated into MattMC.
