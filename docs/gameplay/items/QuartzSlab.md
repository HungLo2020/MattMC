# Quartz Slab

## Obtaining

Quartz Slab can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Quartz Slab is a half-height quartz building block used for floors, paths, roofs, and detail work.

## Behavior

Two slabs can combine into a full block. Slabs can be placed on the upper or lower half of a block space.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:quartz_slab` block.
