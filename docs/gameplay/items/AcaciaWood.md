# Acacia Wood

## Obtaining

Acacia Wood is crafted from Acacia Logs. It can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Acacia Wood is a bark-sided wood block used for building and crafting Acacia Planks.

## Behavior

The block shows bark on every side. Using an axe on it converts it into Stripped Acacia Wood.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_wood` block.
* It appears in the Natural Blocks creative tab.
