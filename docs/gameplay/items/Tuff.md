# Tuff

**Tuff** (`minecraft:tuff`) is the collected starting material for a dark masonry family. [Item registration][items]

## Obtaining and use

Mine Tuff with an **unbroken pickaxe**, including Wood. It drops itself without Silk Touch; Fortune does not increase the count. The [Tuff block guide](../blocks/Tuff.md#obtaining-and-mining) explains the checked natural deposit route. No bundled recipe manufactures raw Tuff. [Loot][loot-tuff] · [Mining tag][pickaxe]

Place it directly, or use the [family crafting recipes](../blocks/Tuff.md#crafting) and [stonecutting shortcuts](../blocks/Tuff.md#stonecutting) for polished, brick, chiseled and shaped forms. Chiseled Tuff and Chiseled Tuff Bricks are distinct products. [Polishing recipe][crafting-polished-tuff] · [Chiseled recipes][crafting-chiseled-tuff] [crafting-chiseled-tuff-bricks]

The [placed guide](../blocks/Tuff.md#placement-and-properties) owns its properties and shared shape behavior. The item is present in Building Blocks and Natural Blocks Creative tabs. [Creative entries][creative]
