# Open Eyeblossom

**Open Eyeblossom** (`minecraft:open_eyeblossom`) is the matching item for the [placed Open Eyeblossom form](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#open-eyeblossom). [Eyeblossom items] · [Display names]

## Obtaining

Break an open plant to collect one Open Eyeblossom. [The acquisition guide](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#finding-and-propagating-eyeblossoms) covers Pale Garden, Grass Block Bone Meal, and a possible trader offer.

## Usage

Use it for Orange Dye, Blindness Suspicious Stew, decoration, or compost. [Exact recipes](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#forms-and-exact-ids) and [Bee poisoning](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#bees-and-harmful-effects) belong to the block guide. Both forms can go in a [Flower Pot](../blocks/FlowerPot.md#supported-plants).

## Behavior

Its placed form [opens and closes with world time](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#opening-closing-and-nearby-plants). It emits no block light, and direct Bone Meal does not duplicate it.
