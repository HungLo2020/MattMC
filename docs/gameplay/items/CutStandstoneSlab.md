# Cut Standstone Slab

## Obtaining

Cut Standstone Slab can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Cut Standstone Slab is a half-height building block used for floors, paths, roofs, and detail work.

## Behavior

Two slabs can combine into a full block. Slabs can be placed on the upper or lower half of a block space.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:cut_standstone_slab`.
