# Bucket of Medium Catfish

## Obtaining

Bucket of Medium Catfish is obtained by using a Water Bucket on a Medium Catfish, or from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Bucket of Medium Catfish is used to carry and release Medium Catfish.

## Behavior

Using the bucket places the contained creature into water and returns an empty Bucket.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:medium_catfish_bucket`.
