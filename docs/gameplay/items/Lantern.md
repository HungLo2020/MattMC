# Lantern

Lantern is the item for **`minecraft:lantern`**. Surround one ordinary Torch with **eight Iron Nuggets** in a Crafting Table to make one Lantern. It emits steady light level **15**. [Item][items] · [Recipe][recipe-lantern] · [Registration][reg-lanterns]

Place it on a suitable floor support or beneath a suitable ceiling. It can be waterlogged, and removing its required support breaks it. Standing and hanging are states of the same item. [Placement and water state][lantern]

**Hand mining returns the Lantern in this source**: it has no required-tool drop gate. A pickaxe speeds breaking. Silk Touch is unnecessary and Fortune adds no extra item. [Registration][reg-lanterns] · [Pickaxe tag][pickaxe] [lantern-tag] · [Loot][loot-lantern] · [Player gate][gate]

See [Lanterns and Soul Lanterns](../blocks/Lanterns.md) for exact support, bucket interactions, Librarian trades, and the separate Soul Lantern's lower brightness.
