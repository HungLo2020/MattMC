# Leaf Litter

**Leaf Litter** (`minecraft:leaf_litter`) is the matching item for the [placed Leaf Litter form](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#leaf-litter). [Cover items] · [Display names]

## Obtaining

Collect **one item per segment** from placed Leaf Litter, including by hand, or smelt leaf-block items. [Finding and making ground cover](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#finding-and-making-ground-cover) gives the exact smelting recipe and a Dark Forest route.

## Usage

Use it as a ground covering, compost it, or burn it as Furnace fuel. The guide lists [compost and fuel values](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#dye-bees-compost-and-fuel).

## Behavior

Leaf Litter needs a **sturdy upper face** beneath it and fills a cell in up to four segments. It cannot be grown with Bone Meal. See [support and coverage](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#ground-coverage-and-facing).
