# Andesite

**Andesite** (`minecraft:andesite`) is a placeable decorative stone with polished, stair, slab and wall forms. Its polished finish has stairs and slabs, but no wall. [Registrations][blocks] [items]

## Obtaining and use

Mine its natural deposits with an **unbroken pickaxe**, including Wood. A placed Andesite block drops itself without Silk Touch; Fortune does not multiply that drop. See the [decorative-stone guide](../blocks/DecorativeStone.md#finding-granite-diorite-and-andesite) for the checked generation route. [Loot][loot-andesite] · [Mining tag][pickaxe]

The [canonical recipes](../blocks/DecorativeStone.md#crafting-the-base-and-polished-blocks) cover making the base block and polishing it. The [shaped variants and stonecutting table](../blocks/DecorativeStone.md#polished-and-shaped-variants) give exact yields. [Base recipe][crafting-andesite] · [Polishing recipe][crafting-polished-andesite]

Place the full block directly for construction, or use the matching shape item. The [placed guide](../blocks/DecorativeStone.md#placement-and-properties) owns placement and mining properties. Andesite appears in both the Building Blocks and Natural Blocks Creative tabs. [Creative entries][creative]
