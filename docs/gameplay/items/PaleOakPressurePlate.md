# Pale Oak Pressure Plate

## Obtaining

Pale Oak Pressure Plate can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Pale Oak Pressure Plate is a redstone input block triggered by entities standing on it.

## Behavior

Pressure plates emit redstone power while they are pressed.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:pale_oak_pressure_plate` block.
