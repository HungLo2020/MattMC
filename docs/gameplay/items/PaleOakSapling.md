# Pale Oak Sapling

## Obtaining

Pale Oak Sapling can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Pale Oak Sapling is used as vegetation, natural decoration, farming, or plant-related building detail.

## Behavior

It can be placed on valid supporting blocks and follows the normal behavior for its plant type.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:pale_oak_sapling` block.
