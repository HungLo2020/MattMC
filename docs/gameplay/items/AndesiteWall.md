# Andesite Wall

## Obtaining

Andesite Walls are crafted from Andesite or obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Andesite Walls are used for stone barriers, supports, posts, and decorative edging.

## Behavior

Walls connect to nearby walls, fences, panes, and many solid blocks. Their shape changes based on neighboring blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:andesite_wall` block.
* It appears in the Building Blocks creative tab.
