# Smoker

A **Smoker** (`minecraft:smoker`) places a three-slot cooking device for **smoking recipes**.

## Obtaining

Craft it with four log-tag ingredients around one Furnace. Use an unbroken pickaxe, including Wood, to recover a placed device. Its loot returns one item and preserves a custom name; inventory contents drop separately.

## Use

Place it, add a matching input and fuel, and leave output space. The current smoking recipes take 100 game ticks, while this device halves the default fuel duration: it cooks those recipes faster than the standard Furnace without doubling items per fuel. It does not accept every smelting recipe.

It is also the Butcher villager job-site block, subject to ordinary employment rules. The [Furnace family guide](../blocks/Furnace.md#smoker) gives the exact crafting pattern, fuel comparison, Hopper access, comparator behavior and collection rules.
