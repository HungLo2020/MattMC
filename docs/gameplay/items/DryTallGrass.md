# Dry Tall Grass

## Obtaining

Dry Tall Grass can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Dry Tall Grass is used as vegetation, natural decoration, or plant-related building detail.

## Behavior

It can be placed on valid supporting blocks and follows the normal behavior for its plant type.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:dry_tall_grass`.
