# Azalea Leaves

## Obtaining

Azalea Leaves are collected with Silk Touch tools or obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Azalea Leaves are used for lush foliage, hedges, and natural decoration.

## Behavior

Leaves decay when they are not supported by a nearby log unless they are player-placed or otherwise persistent.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:azalea_leaves` block.
* It appears in the Natural Blocks creative tab.
