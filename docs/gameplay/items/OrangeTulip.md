# Orange Tulip

## Obtaining

Orange Tulip can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Orange Tulip is used as vegetation, natural decoration, or plant-related building detail.

## Behavior

It can be placed on valid supporting blocks and follows the normal behavior for its plant type.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:orange_tulip` block.
