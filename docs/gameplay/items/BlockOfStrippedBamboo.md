# Block of Stripped Bamboo

## Obtaining

Block of Stripped Bamboo can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Block of Stripped Bamboo is a wood building block and crafting material.

## Behavior

It follows normal wood-block behavior. Axe interactions may strip compatible wood blocks into their stripped form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:stripped_bamboo_block` block.
