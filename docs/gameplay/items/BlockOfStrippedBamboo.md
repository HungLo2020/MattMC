# Block of Stripped Bamboo

**Block of Stripped Bamboo** is the item form of `minecraft:stripped_bamboo_block`. The [block guide](../blocks/BambooBlocks.md#block-of-stripped-bamboo) owns its placed behavior. [Item registration][item]

## Obtaining

Use an unbroken axe on a placed [Block of Bamboo](BlockOfBamboo.md), then mine the stripped block. It has no separate bundled crafting or Stonecutter recipe.

## Usage

Use it for axis-oriented building, [Bamboo Planks](../blocks/WoodConstruction.md#planks-and-materials), [Bamboo Hanging Signs](../blocks/Signs.md#hanging-sign-variants), and [Bamboo Shelves](../blocks/Shelves.md#variants-and-crafting).

## Behavior

It drops one stripped block in ordinary mining, can be collected by hand, and has no further stripping step. See [Bamboo blocks](../blocks/BambooBlocks.md) for placement, tool, fire, and fuel rules.
