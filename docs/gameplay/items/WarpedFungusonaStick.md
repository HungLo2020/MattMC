# Warped Fungus on a Stick

## Obtaining

Warped Fungus on a Stick can be crafted from a Fishing Rod and Warped Fungus. It can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Warped Fungus on a Stick is used to guide and boost a saddled Strider.

## Behavior

Using it while riding a Strider gives the Strider a short speed boost and consumes durability.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:warped_fungus_on_a_stick`.
