# Magenta Carpet

## Obtaining

Magenta Carpet can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Magenta Carpet is a thin decorative floor covering.

## Behavior

Carpets occupy a low layer of the block space and can be placed on top of many blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:magenta_carpet` block.
