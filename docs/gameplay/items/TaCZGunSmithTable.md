# TaCZ Gun Smith Table

## Obtaining

TaCZ Gun Smith Table can be crafted and can also be obtained from the Creative Menu. It is registered as `minecraft:gun_smith_table`.

## Usage

TaCZ Gun Smith Table is used to craft TaCZ firearms from the integrated TaCZ and gun pack recipe set. Place it and open it to browse craftable guns.

## Behavior

The table shows every registered firearm recipe and consumes the listed materials from the player inventory when a gun is crafted.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:gun_smith_table` block.
* This block is part of the integrated TaCZ firearms system.
