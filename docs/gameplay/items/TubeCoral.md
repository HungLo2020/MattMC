# Tube Coral

**Tube Coral** (`minecraft:tube_coral`) places the upright blue living coral plant. It is separate from the full Tube Coral Block and the floor/wall Tube Coral Fan. [Registration][blocks] [items]

## Obtaining

Its block loot requires **Silk Touch**; Shears alone and ordinary hand harvesting give nothing. The living plant has no correct-tool material gate. Find it through [Warm Ocean reefs](../blocks/Coral.md#warm-ocean-reefs), or use the guide's [underwater Bone Meal route](../blocks/Coral.md#bone-meal-for-plants-and-fans). No coral starter is required for that generation route. [Loot][loot-tube-coral] · [Properties][blocks] · [Bone Meal][bonemeal]

## Usage

Place it on a sturdy upper face. Keep it waterlogged or beside qualifying water, and harvest it before removing its support. Drying can turn it into Dead Tube Coral, whose harvesting requires a correct Silk Touch pickaxe. See [Coral placement and harvesting](../blocks/Coral.md#harvesting-coral). [Support/water][plant-base] · [Death][living-plant] · [Dead loot][loot-dead-tube-coral] [pickaxe]
