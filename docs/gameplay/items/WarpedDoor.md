# Warped Door

## Obtaining

Warped Door can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Warped Door is a warped wood or Nether vegetation block item used for building and decoration.

## Behavior

When placed, it creates the `minecraft:warped_door` block and follows the normal placement rules for that warped block type.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:warped_door` block.
