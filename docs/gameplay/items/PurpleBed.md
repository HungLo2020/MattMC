# Purple Bed

## Obtaining

Purple Bed can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Purple Bed is used as a sleeping point and respawn point in valid dimensions.

## Behavior

Using a bed at night or during thunderstorms advances time when all requirements are met. Beds are unsafe to use in some dimensions.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:purple_bed` block.
