# Iron Bars

## Obtaining

Iron Bars can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Iron Bars is an iron utility or building block used for barriers, decoration, doors, or redstone-controlled openings.

## Behavior

Iron doors and trapdoors require redstone to open, while iron bars and chains behave as decorative structural blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:iron_bars` block.
