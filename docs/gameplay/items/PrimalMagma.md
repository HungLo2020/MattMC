# Primal Magma

Primal Magma (`minecraft:primal_magma`) is the inventory form of the dimly glowing [Primal Magma block](../blocks/PrimalMagma.md). It is explicitly available in Creative. No crafting or natural Survival source was established in the reviewed data.

Place it for its block behavior, including light level 5 and conditional hot-floor damage. Its normal placed state is inactive, but that does not disable the active step-on hazard. See the [block guide](../blocks/PrimalMagma.md) before handling it near players or dropped items.

Both Primal Magma and Fissure Primal Magma loot tables name this item. However, their correct-tool requirement has no matching bundled mineable tag, so ordinary pickaxe collection is not established. Fissure is a separate block without a separately registered inventory item.
