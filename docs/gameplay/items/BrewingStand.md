# Brewing Stand

The Brewing Stand item places the workstation used for brewing potions. Its item ID is `minecraft:brewing_stand`.

## Obtaining and use

See the [Brewing Stand block page](../blocks/BrewingStand.md) for the crafting recipe, harvesting rules, slot layout, fuel, and timing. Place the item, then interact with the stand to open its menu.

For Water Bottles, Awkward Potions, and worked potion chains, use the [brewing guide](../brewing/Brewing.md).
