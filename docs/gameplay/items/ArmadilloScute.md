# Armadillo Scute

## Obtaining

Armadillo Scutes are obtained from Armadillos and can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Armadillo Scutes are used to craft and repair Wolf Armor.

## Behavior

The item is a stackable animal material.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:armadillo_scute`.
* It appears in the Ingredients creative tab.
