# Jungle Sapling

## Obtaining

Jungle Sapling can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Jungle Sapling is planted to grow its matching tree.

## Behavior

It grows over time when planted on valid soil with enough light. Bone Meal can be used to try to grow it immediately.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:jungle_sapling` block.
