# Atlatitan Spawn Egg

## Obtaining

The Atlatitan Spawn Egg is available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Atlatitan Spawn Egg on a block to spawn an Atlatitan.

## Behavior

Spawn eggs are creative utility items used to place mobs directly into the world.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:atlatitan_spawn_egg`.
* This mob entry comes from the bundled Alex's Caves content in MattMC.
