# Cyan Wool

## Obtaining

Cyan Wool can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Cyan Wool is a colored textile block used for building, beds, banners, and decoration.

## Behavior

Wool is flammable and can be mined quickly with shears.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:cyan_wool` block.
