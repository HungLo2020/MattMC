# Oxidized Copper Trapdoor

## Obtaining

Oxidized Copper Trapdoor can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Oxidized Copper Trapdoor is placed as a two-block-tall doorway. Interact with it or power it with redstone to open and close it.

## Behavior

It has upper and lower block halves and follows standard door behavior for its material.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:oxidized_copper_trapdoor` block.
