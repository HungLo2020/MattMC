# Green Candle

## Obtaining

Green Candle can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Green Candle is used as a small decorative light source.

## Behavior

Candles can be lit, extinguished, grouped on a block, and placed on compatible cakes.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:green_candle` block.
