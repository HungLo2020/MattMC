# Copper Bars, Chains, and Lanterns

This inventory family contains **24 items**: Copper Bars, Copper Chain, and Copper Lantern, each in four oxidation stages with an additional waxed form of every stage. Each item places its matching block variant. The [placed-fixture guide](../blocks/CopperBarsChainsAndLanterns.md) owns their recipes, support, waterlogging, finish changes, and collection rules. [Item registration][items] · [Eight-item helper][helper]

## Find or craft a variant

Search the displayed name in MattMC's [inventory item browser](../mechanics/InventoryBrowser.md), such as “Weathered Copper Chain” or “Waxed Copper Lantern”. All 24 have ordinary category entries, so this insertion route is available in Survival as well as Creative, subject to the browser's feature, cursor, and inventory-capacity checks. [Building entries][building] · [Functional entries][functional]

For exact item IDs and resource routes, use the family tables:

- [Copper Bars variants](../blocks/CopperBarsChainsAndLanterns.md#bars-variants)
- [Copper Chain variants](../blocks/CopperBarsChainsAndLanterns.md#chain-variants)
- [Copper Lantern variants](../blocks/CopperBarsChainsAndLanterns.md#lantern-variants)

The [crafting section](../blocks/CopperBarsChainsAndLanterns.md#getting-the-pieces) gives the three base layouts and waxing recipes. Matching names do not make Iron materials or ordinary Torches interchangeable ingredients.

## Place and collect

A Lantern's standing or hanging position and a Chain's axis are chosen during placement; they do not have extra inventory IDs. The same applies to bar connections and waterlogging. These states are not saved by the ordinary matching-item drops. [Placed states and collection](../blocks/CopperBarsChainsAndLanterns.md#collecting-and-moving-fixtures)
