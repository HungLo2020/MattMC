# Oak Button

## Obtaining

Oak Button can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Oak Button is a redstone input block. Place it on a surface and press it to emit a short redstone pulse.

## Behavior

As a wooden button, it releases automatically after a brief delay and can also be triggered by projectiles.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:oak_button` block.
