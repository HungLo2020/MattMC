# Pewen Door

## Obtaining

Pewen Door can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Pewen Door is placed as a two-block-tall doorway. Interact with it or power it with redstone to open and close it.

## Behavior

It has upper and lower block halves and follows standard door behavior for its material.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:pewen_door`.
