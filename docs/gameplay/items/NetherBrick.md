# Nether Brick

**Nether Brick** (`minecraft:nether_brick`, singular) is a small crafting item. It is **not the placeable Nether Bricks block** and cannot be placed as that block by itself. [Plain item registration][items]

## Obtaining and use

Smelt Netherrack in a Furnace to obtain it. Adult Piglin bartering can also select a stack of Nether Brick items. The [Nether Bricks guide](../blocks/NetherBricks.md#make-the-small-brick-item) owns the exact recipe, timing and checked barter count. [Smelting][smelting-nether-brick] · [Barter loot][barter-loot]

Use it in the [canonical masonry recipes](../blocks/NetherBricks.md#crafting-and-cracking) for regular or Red Nether Bricks and Nether Brick Fences. The fence recipe combines small Nether Brick items with full Nether Bricks blocks, so keep the two item types distinct. [Regular block][crafting-nether-bricks] · [Red block][crafting-red-nether-bricks] · [Fence][crafting-nether-brick-fence]
