# Resin Brick Wall

## Obtaining

Resin Brick Wall can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Resin Brick Wall is used for barriers, posts, edging, and decorative detail.

## Behavior

It connects to nearby compatible blocks and changes shape based on neighboring blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:resin_brick_wall` block.
