# Dead Brain Coral Fan

## Obtaining

Dead Brain Coral Fan can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Dead Brain Coral Fan is used for aquatic or reef-themed decoration.

## Behavior

Dead coral variants are inert decorative blocks and do not need water to remain in their dead form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:dead_brain_coral_fan` block.
