# Basalt

**Basalt** (`minecraft:basalt`) is a placeable block whose axis follows the clicked face. Polished Basalt also has this orientation control; Smooth Basalt does not. [Registrations][blocks] [items] · [Axis placement][pillar]

## Obtaining and use

Use an **unbroken pickaxe**, including Wood, to collect one Basalt from its placed block. Silk Touch is unnecessary and Fortune does not multiply the drop. The [placed guide](../blocks/BlackstoneAndBasalt.md#finding-blackstone-and-basalt) covers a checked natural route, and its [lava section](../blocks/BlackstoneAndBasalt.md#making-basalt-with-lava) explains the active conversion rule. [Loot][loot-basalt] · [Mining tag][pickaxe]

Follow [Basalt variants and orientation](../blocks/BlackstoneAndBasalt.md#basalt-variants-and-orientation) for the polished and smooth recipes. Polishing uses crafting or stonecutting; making Smooth Basalt uses Furnace smelting. [Polishing][crafting-polished-basalt] · [Smoothing][smelting-smooth-basalt]

Related: [Polished Basalt](PolishedBasalt.md) · [Smooth Basalt](SmoothBasalt.md) · [Items](Items.md)
