# Orange Glazed Terracotta

## Obtaining

Orange Glazed Terracotta can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Orange Glazed Terracotta is a patterned decorative block used in floors, walls, and ornamental builds.

## Behavior

Its pattern orientation depends on placement direction.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:orange_glazed_terracotta` block.
