# Fern

**Fern** (`minecraft:fern`) is the matching item for the [placed Fern form](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#fern). [Small items] · [Display names]

## Obtaining

Use **Shears on a Fern**, or on an intact Large Fern for two Fern items. Taiga plants, a possible Wandering Trader offer, and Taiga village-house chest loot are covered in [the acquisition guide](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#finding-plants-and-tall-inventory-items).

## Usage

Plant and [multiply it with Bone Meal and Shears](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#bone-meal-and-a-renewable-supply), display it in a [Flower Pot](../blocks/FlowerPot.md#supported-plants), compost it, or use it for [Fern Thatch](../blocks/FloodBasaltAndFernThatch.md#fern-thatch-crafting-and-collecting).

## Behavior

Fern is the one-block form. See [the block guide](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#planting-and-tall-halves) for its soil and growth conditions.
