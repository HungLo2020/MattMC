# Stripped Dark Oak Log

## Obtaining

Stripped Dark Oak Log can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Stripped Dark Oak Log is a wood-family building block and crafting material.

## Behavior

It follows normal wood-family behavior. Axe interactions may strip compatible wood blocks into their stripped form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:stripped_dark_oak_log` block.
