# Purple Concrete Powder

## Obtaining

Purple Concrete Powder can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Purple Concrete Powder is a colored building block that can be converted into concrete.

## Behavior

Concrete powder falls like sand and hardens into concrete when it contacts water.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:purple_concrete_powder` block.
