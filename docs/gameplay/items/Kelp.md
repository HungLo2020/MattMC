# Kelp

## Obtaining

Kelp can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Kelp is an underwater plant used for decoration, farming, and smelting into Dried Kelp.

## Behavior

Kelp grows underwater and follows normal aquatic plant placement and growth rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:kelp` block.
