# Diamond

Diamond (`minecraft:diamond`) is a directly usable gem for tools, equipment, and selected workstations. Unlike Raw Iron or Raw Copper, it does not need an ingot-processing step.

## Mining and storage

Use an **Iron, Diamond, or Netherite Pickaxe** on Diamond Ore or Deepslate Diamond Ore to collect the ordinary gem drop. Stone and Copper Pickaxes are not sufficient. The base loot is one Diamond; Fortune modifies that branch, while Silk Touch selects the ore block instead.

Nine Diamonds in a 3 × 3 square make a Block of Diamond; shapelessly unpacking it returns nine. This is a storage conversion, not a method of making new gems.

See [ore resources](../blocks/OreResources.md) for the checked harvesting scope. This page does not promise a best mining height or an exhaustive chest-loot list.

## Selected uses

- **Diamond Pickaxe:** three Diamonds across the top above two centered Sticks, using the diamond-tool-material tag
- **Enchanting Table:** a Book above a row of Diamond–Obsidian–Diamond, with three Obsidian below
- **Anvil repair:** Diamond is the material in the Diamond tool repair tag; the repair cost and amount follow [Anvil mechanics](../mechanics/AnvilMechanics.md)
- **Netherite equipment progression:** a Diamond item of the required type is the base for its matching upgrade; raw loose Diamonds are not a substitute for the equipment. See [Smithing](../smithing/Smithing.md)

These are selected uses, not every Diamond recipe. Choose what to craft before committing limited supplies.
