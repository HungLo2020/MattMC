# Dark Oak Leaves

## Obtaining

Dark Oak Leaves can be collected with Silk Touch tools or obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Dark Oak Leaves is used for foliage, tree canopies, hedges, landscaping, and natural decoration.

## Behavior

Leaves can decay when they are not supported by nearby logs unless they are player-placed or otherwise persistent.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:dark_oak_leaves` block.
