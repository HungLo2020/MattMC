# Chiseled Sandstone

**Chiseled Sandstone** (`minecraft:chiseled_sandstone`) places the chiseled decorative full block in the pale Sandstone family.

## Obtaining

Craft **2 [Sandstone Slab](SandstoneSlab.md)s stacked vertically into 1 Chiseled Sandstone**, or stonecut **1 ordinary [Sandstone](Sandstone.md) into 1 Chiseled Sandstone**. The crafting recipe uses the base slab of this color; Cut and Smooth slabs do not substitute. [Crafting][recipe-1] · [Stonecutting][recipe-2]

Mine with an **unbroken pickaxe, including Wood**. In ordinary mining, the placed block drops **1 Chiseled Sandstone item**. Hand mining does not collect the normal drop. Silk Touch is unnecessary and Fortune adds no multiplier. [Exact loot][loot] · [Family mining rules](../blocks/Sandstone.md#obtaining-and-mining)

## Usage

Use it as a full building block, or as an alternative ingredient when crafting ordinary [Sandstone Slab](SandstoneSlab.md)s and [Sandstone Stairs](SandstoneStairs.md). It can share those crafting patterns with the other accepted finishes of the same color; see the [exact family recipe table](../blocks/Sandstone.md#crafting-finishes-and-shapes).

## Behavior

The full block has no selectable facing or pillar axis and stays in place when support underneath is removed. Mining it with the correct tool keeps the Chiseled finish. See [Sandstone placement and use](../blocks/Sandstone.md#placement-and-use).
