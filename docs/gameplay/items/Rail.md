# Rail

The Rail item places `minecraft:rail`, the track block that can form straight sections, slopes, and corners for minecarts.

## Obtaining and use

Craft **16 Rails** from six Iron Ingots and one Stick using the [Transport recipe layout](../mechanics/Transport.md#building-a-basic-rail-route). Ordinary Survival mining returns the rail without a correct-tool requirement; a pickaxe is its tagged mining tool.

Place rails on supported upper surfaces and inspect their connections. Use ordinary Rail for corners because the three special rail types cannot curve. The [Rails block guide](../blocks/Rails.md) covers support, slopes, junctions, and special-rail choices. [Transport](../mechanics/Transport.md) covers practical routes and stations.
