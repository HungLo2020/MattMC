# Lapis Lazuli Ore

## Obtaining

Lapis Lazuli Ore can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Lapis Lazuli Ore is mined as a resource block.

## Behavior

It requires the correct tool for drops and yields its associated resource or ore drop according to loot rules.

## Notes
