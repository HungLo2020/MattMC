# Closed Eyeblossom

**Closed Eyeblossom** (`minecraft:closed_eyeblossom`) is the matching item for the [placed Closed Eyeblossom form](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#closed-eyeblossom). [Eyeblossom items] · [Display names]

## Obtaining

Break a closed plant to collect one Closed Eyeblossom. [The acquisition guide](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#finding-and-propagating-eyeblossoms) covers Pale Garden, Grass Block Bone Meal, and changing an Open Eyeblossom through world time.

## Usage

Use it for Gray Dye, Nausea Suspicious Stew, decoration, or compost. See [the exact recipes](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#forms-and-exact-ids). It can also go in a [Flower Pot](../blocks/FlowerPot.md#supported-plants).

## Behavior

The planted form [opens and closes with world time](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#opening-closing-and-nearby-plants). The closed form is absent from the bundled [Bee food and attraction tags](../blocks/Eyeblossoms.md#bees-and-harmful-effects), and direct Bone Meal does not duplicate it.
