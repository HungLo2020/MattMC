# Ender Chest

## Obtaining

Ender Chest can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Ender Chest is a storage block linked to the player's personal Ender Chest inventory.

## Behavior

Each player sees their own contents, and the inventory persists across Ender Chests.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:ender_chest` block.
