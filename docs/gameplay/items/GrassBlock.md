# Grass Block

**Grass Block** (`minecraft:grass_block`) is a terrain block item. The [Soil, Sand, and Gravel guide](../blocks/SoilSandAndGravel.md) covers its placed behavior, support, conversions, and collection. [Item registration][registration] · [Block registration][block-registration]

## Collecting and moving it

Use **Silk Touch** to retain a Grass Block when mining it. Without Silk Touch, the ordinary result is **1 Dirt**, even when using a shovel. [Block loot][loot]

A shovel is the efficient mining tool. No tool material tier is required for this block’s ordinary loot. [Mining tag][shovel-tag] · [Player harvest gate][player]

## Placed uses

The placed block has [cover-survival and light-dependent spreading rules](../blocks/SoilSandAndGravel.md#grass-block-and-mycelium). Bone Meal grows vegetation above it; a hoe makes Farmland and a shovel makes a Dirt Path under their separate conditions.

Follow the shared guide for the exact conditions and recipe sources. [Soil, Sand, and Gravel](../blocks/SoilSandAndGravel.md) · [Blocks](../blocks/Blocks.md) · [Items](Items.md)
