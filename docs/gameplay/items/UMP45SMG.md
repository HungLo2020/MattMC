# UMP45 SMG

Its flat design makes the enemy's lifeline also flat.

## Obtaining

UMP45 SMG can be obtained from the Creative Menu. It is registered as `minecraft:ump45`.

## Usage

UMP45 SMG is a TaCZ firearm. It uses [.45 ACP Bullet](45ACPBullet.md) and fires with the TaCZ gun controls. Reload it with the reload key and aim down sights with the aim key.

* 30 blocks: 9
* 55 blocks: 7
* infinite blocks: 5.5
