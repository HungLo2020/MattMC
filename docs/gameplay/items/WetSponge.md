# Wet Sponge

## Obtaining

Wet Sponge can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Wet Sponge is a sponge block that has absorbed water.

## Behavior

It can be dried through normal sponge-drying mechanics to recover a dry Sponge.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:wet_sponge` block.
