# Dead Fire Coral Block

## Obtaining

Dead Fire Coral Block can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Dead Fire Coral Block is used for aquatic or reef-themed decoration.

## Behavior

Dead coral variants are inert decorative blocks and do not need water to remain in their dead form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:dead_fire_coral_block` block.
