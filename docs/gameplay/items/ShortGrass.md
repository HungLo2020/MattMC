# Short Grass

**Short Grass** (`minecraft:short_grass`) is the matching item for the [placed Short Grass form](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#short-grass). [Small items] · [Display names]

## Obtaining

Use **Shears on Short Grass**, or on an intact Tall Grass plant for two Short Grass items. [Finding plants](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#finding-plants-and-tall-inventory-items) covers checked natural and chest routes.

## Usage

Plant it for decoration, use its [Bone Meal and Shears loop](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#bone-meal-and-a-renewable-supply), or compost it.

## Behavior

This is the **one-block** grass. Its [support and placement](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#planting-and-tall-halves) and [seed/Fortune drops](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#seeds-and-harvesting) are documented in the block guide.
