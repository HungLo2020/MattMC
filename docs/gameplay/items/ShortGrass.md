# Short Grass

## Obtaining

Short Grass can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Short Grass is used as vegetation, natural decoration, farming, or plant-related building detail.

## Behavior

It can be placed on valid supporting blocks and follows the normal behavior for its plant type.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:short_grass` block.
