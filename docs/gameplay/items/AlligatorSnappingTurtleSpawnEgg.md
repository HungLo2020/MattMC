# Alligator Snapping Turtle Spawn Egg

## Obtaining

The Alligator Snapping Turtle Spawn Egg is available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Alligator Snapping Turtle Spawn Egg on a block to spawn an Alligator Snapping Turtle.

## Behavior

Spawn eggs are creative utility items. They are used to place mobs directly into the world.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:alligator_snapping_turtle_spawn_egg`.
* This mob entry comes from the bundled Alex's Caves content in MattMC.
