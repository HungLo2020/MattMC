# Mangrove Planks

## Obtaining

Mangrove Planks can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Mangrove Planks is a core wood building block and crafting ingredient.

## Behavior

It behaves like standard wooden construction material and can be used in many wood recipes.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:mangrove_planks` block.
