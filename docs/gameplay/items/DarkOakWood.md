# Dark Oak Wood

## Obtaining

Dark Oak Wood can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Dark Oak Wood is a wood building block and crafting material.

## Behavior

It follows normal wood-block behavior. Axe interactions may strip compatible wood blocks into their stripped form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:dark_oak_wood` block.
