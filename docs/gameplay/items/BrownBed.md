# Brown Bed

## Obtaining

Brown Bed can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Brown Bed is used as a sleeping point and respawn point in valid dimensions.

## Behavior

Using a bed at night or during thunderstorms advances time when all requirements are met. Beds are unsafe to use in some dimensions.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:brown_bed` block.
