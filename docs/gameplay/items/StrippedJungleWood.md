# Stripped Jungle Wood

## Obtaining

Stripped Jungle Wood can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Stripped Jungle Wood is a wood-family building block and crafting material.

## Behavior

It follows normal wood-family behavior. Axe interactions may strip compatible wood blocks into their stripped form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:stripped_jungle_wood` block.
