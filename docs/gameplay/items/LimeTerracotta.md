# Lime Terracotta

## Obtaining

Lime Terracotta can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Lime Terracotta is a colored clay building block used for walls, floors, roofs, and accents.

## Behavior

It behaves as a solid decorative block.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:lime_terracotta` block.
