# Crimson Sign

## Obtaining

Crimson Sign can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Crimson Sign is used to display editable text in the world.

## Behavior

It keeps its written text after placement and can be dyed or waxed to control editing.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:crimson_sign` block.
