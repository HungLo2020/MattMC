# Yellow Wool

## Obtaining

Yellow Wool can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Yellow Wool is a yellow-colored block item used for building, decoration, storage, lighting, or furnishing depending on its block type.

## Behavior

When placed, it creates the `minecraft:yellow_wool` block and follows the normal behavior for that yellow variant.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:yellow_wool` block.
