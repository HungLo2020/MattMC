# Dark Oak Sapling

## Obtaining

Dark Oak Sapling can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Dark Oak Sapling is used as vegetation, natural decoration, or plant-related building detail.

## Behavior

It can be placed on valid supporting blocks and follows the normal behavior for its plant type.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:dark_oak_sapling` block.
