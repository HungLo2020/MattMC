# Waxed Exposed Copper Grate

## Obtaining

Waxed Exposed Copper Grate can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Waxed Exposed Copper Grate is a copper-family block item used for building, decoration, storage, redstone, or lighting depending on the block type.

## Behavior

When placed, it creates the `minecraft:waxed_exposed_copper_grate` block. Waxed variants keep their oxidation state, while unwaxed weathered variants represent their current copper age.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:waxed_exposed_copper_grate` block.
