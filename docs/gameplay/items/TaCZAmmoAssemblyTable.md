# TaCZ Ammo Assembly Table

## Obtaining

TaCZ Ammo Assembly Table can be crafted and can also be obtained from the Creative Menu. It is registered as `minecraft:ammo_workbench`.

## Usage

TaCZ Ammo Assembly Table is used to craft ammunition for TaCZ firearms from the integrated TaCZ and gun pack recipe set. Place it and open it to browse craftable ammo.

## Behavior

The table shows every registered ammo recipe and consumes the listed materials from the player inventory when ammo is crafted.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:ammo_workbench` block.
* This block is part of the integrated TaCZ firearms system.
