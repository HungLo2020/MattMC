# Wildflowers

**Wildflowers** (`minecraft:wildflowers`) is the matching item for the [placed Wildflowers form](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#wildflowers). [Cover items] · [Display names]

## Obtaining

Break a Wildflowers flowerbed to recover **one item per segment**, without Shears. Birch Forest, Meadow, Bone Meal, and a possible trader offer are covered in [the acquisition guide](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#finding-and-making-ground-cover).

## Usage

Place or fill a flowerbed, [multiply it with Bone Meal](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#bone-meal), craft Yellow Dye, feed Bees, or compost it. See [the exact uses](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#dye-bees-compost-and-fuel).

## Behavior

Up to four items share one cell. The block guide owns [ground support and facing](../blocks/FlowerbedsAndLeafLitter.md#ground-coverage-and-facing).
