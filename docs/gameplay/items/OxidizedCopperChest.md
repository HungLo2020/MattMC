# Oxidized Copper Chest

## Obtaining

Oxidized Copper Chest can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Oxidized Copper Chest is a fully oxidized copper-family block used for building, decoration, redstone, or utility depending on the variant.

## Behavior

Oxidized copper blocks represent the final oxidation state and follow their normal mining, placement, and interaction rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:oxidized_copper_chest` block.
