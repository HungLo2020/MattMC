# Tarantula Hawk Spawn Egg

## Obtaining

The Tarantula Hawk Spawn Egg is available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Tarantula Hawk Spawn Egg on a block to spawn a Tarantula Hawk.

## Behavior

Spawn eggs are creative utility items. They do not have durability and are primarily used for testing, mapmaking, creative building, and quickly placing mobs into the world.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:tarantula_hawk_spawn_egg`.
* This mob entry comes from bundled mod content integrated into MattMC.
