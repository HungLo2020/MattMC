# Crimson Slab

## Obtaining

Place **3 [Crimson Planks](CrimsonPlanks.md) in one horizontal row** in a [Crafting Table](../blocks/CraftingTable.md) to make **6 Crimson Slabs**. Every slot uses Crimson Planks. [Recipe][recipe]

Ordinary mining returns **1 Crimson Slab from a single slab or 2 from a double slab**. You can collect them by hand; an unbroken axe mines them faster. See [mining and drops](../blocks/WoodConstruction.md#mining-and-drops). [Loot table][loot]

## Usage

Use Crimson Slabs for half-height floors, paths, roofs, and trim.

Crimson Slab items are not furnace fuel, and ordinary fire spread does not consume the placed block. See [fire and fuel differences](../blocks/WoodConstruction.md#fire-and-furnace-fuel).

## Behavior

A single Crimson Slab can occupy the upper or lower half of a block space and can be waterlogged. Only another **Crimson Slab** can fill its missing half to form a double slab. Combining the pair clears waterlogging, and the double slab cannot be waterlogged. See [slab placement](../blocks/WoodConstruction.md#slabs) and [waterlogging](../blocks/WoodConstruction.md#waterlogging-and-power) for the shared rules.
