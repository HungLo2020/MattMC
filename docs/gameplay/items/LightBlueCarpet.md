# Light Blue Carpet

## Obtaining

Light Blue Carpet can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Light Blue Carpet is a thin decorative floor covering.

## Behavior

Carpets occupy a low layer of the block space and can be placed on top of many blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:light_blue_carpet` block.
