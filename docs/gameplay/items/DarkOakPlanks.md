# Dark Oak Planks

## Obtaining

Dark Oak Planks can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Dark Oak Planks is a core wood building block and crafting ingredient.

## Behavior

It behaves like standard wooden construction material and can be used in many wood recipes.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:dark_oak_planks` block.
