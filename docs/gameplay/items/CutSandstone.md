# Cut Sandstone

**Cut Sandstone** (`minecraft:cut_sandstone`) places the Cut full-block finish in the pale Sandstone family.

## Obtaining

Craft **4 ordinary [Sandstone](Sandstone.md) in a 2 × 2 square into 4 Cut Sandstone**, or stonecut **1 ordinary Sandstone into 1 Cut Sandstone**. Both routes preserve the number of full blocks. [Crafting][recipe-1] · [Stonecutting][recipe-2]

Mine with an **unbroken pickaxe, including Wood**. In ordinary mining, the placed block drops **1 Cut Sandstone item**. Hand mining does not collect the normal drop. Silk Touch is unnecessary and Fortune adds no multiplier. [Exact loot][loot] · [Family mining rules](../blocks/Sandstone.md#obtaining-and-mining)

## Usage

Use it for full-block building detail or make [Cut Sandstone Slab](CutSandstoneSlab.md)s. It also substitutes for ordinary Sandstone in the crafting recipe for [Sandstone Stairs](SandstoneStairs.md), including mixtures with other accepted finishes of this color. That stair substitution applies to crafting; the Stonecutter uses ordinary Sandstone for those stairs. See the [family recipe table](../blocks/Sandstone.md#crafting-finishes-and-shapes).

## Behavior

The full block has no selectable facing or pillar axis and stays in place when support underneath is removed. Mining it with the correct tool keeps the Cut finish. See [Sandstone placement and use](../blocks/Sandstone.md#placement-and-use).
