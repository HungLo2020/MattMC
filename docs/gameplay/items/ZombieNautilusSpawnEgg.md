# Zombie Nautilus Spawn Egg

## Obtaining

The Zombie Nautilus Spawn Egg is available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Zombie Nautilus Spawn Egg on a block to spawn a Zombie Nautilus.

## Behavior

Spawn eggs are creative utility items used to place mobs directly into the world.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:zombie_nautilus_spawn_egg`.
* It appears in the Spawn Eggs creative tab.
