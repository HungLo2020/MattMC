# Armor Stand

## Obtaining

Armor Stands are crafted from Sticks and a Smooth Stone Slab. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Armor Stand is used to display armor, held items, and decorative equipment setups.

## Behavior

Armor Stands are entities when placed. They can be equipped, broken, moved by some forces, and customized through commands.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:armor_stand`.
* It appears in Functional Blocks and Combat.
