# Cut Copper Stairs

## Obtaining

Cut Copper Stairs can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Cut Copper Stairs is used for stairways, rooflines, seating, trim, and angled details.

## Behavior

Stairs connect into corner shapes depending on nearby stair blocks and placement direction.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:cut_copper_stairs` block.
