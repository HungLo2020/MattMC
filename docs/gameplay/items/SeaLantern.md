# Sea Lantern

Sea Lantern is the item for **`minecraft:sea_lantern`**, a full light-level-**15** block. Craft **four Prismarine Shards in the corners and five Prismarine Crystals elsewhere** in a 3 × 3 grid to make one. [Item][items] · [Registration][reg-sea] · [Recipe][recipe-sea_lantern]

Use Silk Touch to recover a placed Sea Lantern. Otherwise it gives **2–3 Prismarine Crystals** before Fortune, capped at **five** with Fortune; it does not refund the Shards. There is no required-tool-tier drop gate. [Loot][loot-sea_lantern] · [Registration][reg-sea] · [Harvest gate][gate]

Ocean Monument pieces are a verified source of placed Sea Lanterns. They also work as solid underwater lighting blocks without having a waterlogged state. Follow the [Sea Lantern block guide](../blocks/LuminousBlocks.md#sea-lantern) for acquisition, Fortune ranges, and reuse. [Monument generator][monument] · [Placed lights][monument-pieces]
