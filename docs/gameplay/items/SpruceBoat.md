# Spruce Boat

## Obtaining

Spruce Boat is crafted from matching wood materials and can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Spruce Boat is used for travel across water. Place it, enter it, and steer it like other boats.

## Behavior

It can carry riders and can be broken to return itself as an item. Chest variants also provide mobile storage and drop their contents when broken.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:spruce_boat`.
