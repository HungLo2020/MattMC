# Mangrove Roots

## Obtaining

Mangrove Roots can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Mangrove Roots is a wood-family building block and crafting material.

## Behavior

It follows normal wood or root block behavior. Axe interactions may strip compatible wood blocks into their stripped form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:mangrove_roots` block.
