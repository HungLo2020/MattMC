# Water Bucket

## Obtaining

Water Buckets can be obtained by using a Bucket on a water source block. They are also available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Water Bucket to place water, collect it back into a bucket, extinguish fires, move water into builds, or interact with mechanics that depend on water.

## Behavior

Using it places a water source where valid and converts the item back into an empty Bucket.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:water_bucket`.
