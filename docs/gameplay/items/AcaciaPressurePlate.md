# Acacia Pressure Plate

## Obtaining

Acacia Pressure Plates are crafted from Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Acacia Pressure Plate is a redstone input block triggered by entities standing on it.

## Behavior

As a wooden pressure plate, it detects players, mobs, dropped items, and other entities.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_pressure_plate` block.
* It appears with other acacia building blocks in the Building Blocks creative tab.
