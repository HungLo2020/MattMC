# Netherrack

**Netherrack** is the item form of `minecraft:netherrack`. The [block guide](../blocks/Netherrack.md#netherrack) owns its placed behavior. [Item registration][item]

## Obtaining

Collect [Nether terrain or the checked Nylium drops](../blocks/Netherrack.md#finding-and-collecting) with an unbroken pickaxe. There is no bundled crafting recipe producing Netherrack.

## Usage

Use it for building, [small Nether Brick production](../blocks/NetherBricks.md#make-the-small-brick-item), [template duplication](../blocks/Netherrack.md#processing-and-crafting), or [Nylium conversion](../blocks/NetherGroundAndVegetation.md#bone-meal-on-netherrack).

## Behavior

Ordinary successful mining gives one Netherrack. It can support persistent fire, but its item is not default Furnace fuel. See the [Netherrack block guide](../blocks/Netherrack.md).
