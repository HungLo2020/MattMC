# Quartz Stairs

## Obtaining

Quartz Stairs can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Quartz Stairs is used for stairways, rooflines, seating, trim, and angled quartz details.

## Behavior

Stairs connect into corner shapes depending on nearby stair blocks and placement direction.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:quartz_stairs` block.
