# Wither Skeleton Skull

## Obtaining

Wither Skeleton Skull can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Wither Skeleton Skull is a decorative head block used for building, display, mapmaking, and themed decoration.

## Behavior

It can be placed as a block and follows the normal placement behavior for skull-type blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:wither_skeleton_skull` block.
