# Stone

**Stone** (`minecraft:stone`) is the placeable base stone and an ingredient for stone-brick construction and several workstations or redstone components. [Item registration][items]

## Obtaining and use

Collect Stone with an **unbroken Silk Touch pickaxe**, or smelt Cobblestone in a Furnace. Ordinary pickaxe mining of Stone gives Cobblestone instead. The [Stone block guide](../blocks/Stone.md#obtaining) owns the collection and smelting details. [Loot][loot-stone] · [Smelting][smelting-stone] · [Mining tag][pickaxe]

Place it as a full building block, or follow the [family recipes](../blocks/Stone.md#uses) for Stone Bricks, shaped blocks, Stone Buttons and Stone Pressure Plates. **Smooth Stone is a separate item**, made by smelting Stone again. [Brick recipe][crafting-stone-bricks] · [Smooth Stone recipe][smelting-smooth-stone]
