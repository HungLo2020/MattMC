# Quartz Pillar

## Obtaining

Quartz Pillar can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Quartz Pillar is a quartz-family decorative building block.

## Behavior

It behaves as a solid decorative block and follows normal quartz block mining and placement rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:quartz_pillar` block.
