# Tripwire Hook

The Tripwire Hook item places `minecraft:tripwire_hook`, a wall-mounted endpoint and redstone output for a String detection line.

## Obtaining and use

Craft two Hooks from one Iron Ingot, one Stick, and one accepted Planks arranged vertically in that order. See the [canonical recipe and collection guide](../blocks/Tripwire.md#obtaining-and-collecting). Ordinary Survival mining returns the Hook without a specific tool requirement.

Mount two Hooks on sturdy facing wall surfaces and connect them with a straight line of [String](String.md). A complete eligible line can power both Hooks when crossed. The [Tripwire guide](../blocks/Tripwire.md) covers the 1–40 String-segment limit, attachment, output, rechecks, and breaking with Shears to suppress a cut-wire trigger.
