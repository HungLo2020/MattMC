# Magenta Banner

## Obtaining

Magenta Banner can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Magenta Banner is a decorative banner block that can display patterns and color-themed designs.

## Behavior

Banners can be placed on the ground or walls and can be customized with banner patterns and dyes.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:magenta_banner` block.
