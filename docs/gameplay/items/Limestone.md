# Limestone

Limestone is the placeable item for the [Limestone building family](../blocks/Limestone.md), registered as `minecraft:limestone`.

## Obtaining and use

It is included in Creative inventory. Crafting, generation, and a reliable Survival harvesting tool are not established in the checked MattMC implementation. The old assumption that any stone-like block can simply be mined with a pickaxe does not substitute for this block's missing tool-tag wiring.

Place it for full-block construction. The family guide documents the stairs, slabs, walls, smooth, pillar, and chiseled forms and their current limitations. No separate item-only action is registered here.
