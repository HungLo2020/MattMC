# White Shulker Box

## Obtaining

White Shulker Box can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

White Shulker Box is a white-colored block item used for building, decoration, storage, lighting, or furnishing depending on its block type.

## Behavior

When placed, it creates the `minecraft:white_shulker_box` block and follows the normal behavior for that white variant.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:white_shulker_box` block.
