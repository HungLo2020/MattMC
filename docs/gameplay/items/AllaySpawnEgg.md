# Allay Spawn Egg

## Obtaining

The Allay Spawn Egg is available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Allay Spawn Egg on a block to spawn an Allay.

## Behavior

Spawn eggs are creative utility items. They do not have durability and are primarily used for testing, building, mapmaking, and creative play.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:allay_spawn_egg`.
* It appears in the Spawn Eggs creative tab.
