# Acacia Fence

## Obtaining

Acacia Fences are crafted from Acacia Planks and Sticks. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Acacia Fences are used as barriers, railings, animal pens, and decorative trim.

## Behavior

Fence blocks connect to nearby fences, fence gates, and many solid blocks. They are taller than they visually appear for collision, making them useful for keeping mobs contained.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_fence` block.
* It appears with other acacia building blocks in the Building Blocks creative tab.
