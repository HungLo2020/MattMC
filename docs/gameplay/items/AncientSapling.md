# Ancient Sapling

## Obtaining

Ancient Saplings can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from any Alex's Caves survival sources enabled in the current build.

## Usage

The Ancient Sapling is planted to grow Ancient Trees.

## Behavior

It uses the Ancient Tree grower from the bundled Alex's Caves content. Bone Meal can be used to attempt growth when conditions are valid.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:ancient_sapling` block.
* It appears in the Natural Blocks creative tab.
