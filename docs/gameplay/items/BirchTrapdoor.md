# Birch Trapdoor

## Obtaining

Birch Trapdoor can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Birch Trapdoor is placed as a two-block-tall doorway. Interact with it or power it with redstone to open and close it.

## Behavior

It has upper and lower block halves and follows standard wooden door behavior.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:birch_trapdoor` block.
