# Smooth Red Sandstone

**Smooth Red Sandstone** (`minecraft:smooth_red_sandstone`) places the Smooth full-block finish and supplies the matching Smooth stairs and slabs.

## Obtaining

Smelt **1 ordinary [Red Sandstone](RedSandstone.md) into 1 Smooth Red Sandstone** in a [Furnace](../blocks/Furnace.md). The recipe takes **200 ticks**, nominally 10 seconds at 20 ticks per second, and specifies **0.1 recipe experience** per input. Cut and Chiseled blocks do not substitute. See the Furnace guide for fuel and experience collection. [Smelting][recipe-1]

Mine with an **unbroken pickaxe, including Wood**. In ordinary mining, the placed block drops **1 Smooth Red Sandstone item**. Hand mining does not collect the normal drop. Silk Touch is unnecessary and Fortune adds no multiplier. [Exact loot][loot] · [Family mining rules](../blocks/Sandstone.md#obtaining-and-mining)

## Usage

Build with the full block or turn it into [Smooth Red Sandstone Stairs](SmoothRedSandstoneStairs.md) and [Smooth Red Sandstone Slab](SmoothRedSandstoneSlab.md)s through crafting or stonecutting. Stonecutting gives more stairs per full block; the [family recipe table](../blocks/Sandstone.md#crafting-finishes-and-shapes) and [cutting yields](../blocks/Sandstone.md#stonecutting) compare the routes.

## Behavior

The full block has no selectable facing or pillar axis and stays in place when support underneath is removed. Mining it with the correct tool keeps the Smooth finish. See [Sandstone placement and use](../blocks/Sandstone.md#placement-and-use).
