# Brick

A **Brick** (`minecraft:brick`) is a fired crafting ingredient. It is distinct from the placeable **[Bricks block](Bricks.md)** and cannot be placed directly. [Registration][ingredients]

## Obtaining and use

Smelt **Clay Balls** using the [Brick smelting recipe](../blocks/ClayAndBricks.md#crafting-and-smelting). That guide gives the exact output, cooking time, recipe XP, and fuel planning. Smelting the Clay block itself follows the separate Terracotta recipe. [Brick recipe][brick-recipe]

Two checked uses are [crafting Bricks blocks](../blocks/ClayAndBricks.md#crafting-and-smelting) for construction and [crafting Flower Pots](../blocks/FlowerPot.md#crafting) for plant displays. Follow those canonical recipe sections for their arrangements. [Bricks recipe][bricks-recipe] · [Flower Pot recipe][pot-recipe]
