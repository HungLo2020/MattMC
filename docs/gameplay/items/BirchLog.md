# Birch Log

## Obtaining

Birch Log can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Birch Log is a wood building block and crafting material.

## Behavior

It follows normal wood-block behavior. Axe interactions may strip compatible wood blocks into their stripped form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:birch_log` block.
