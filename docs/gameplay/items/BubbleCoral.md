# Bubble Coral

## Obtaining

Bubble Coral can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Bubble Coral is used for aquatic decoration and reef-themed builds.

## Behavior

Coral blocks and plants require water to remain alive; without water, many coral variants turn into dead coral.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:bubble_coral` block.
