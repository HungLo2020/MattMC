# Horn Coral Block

## Obtaining

Horn Coral Block can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Horn Coral Block is used for aquatic decoration and reef-themed builds.

## Behavior

Coral blocks and plants require water to remain alive; without water, many coral variants turn into dead coral.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:horn_coral_block` block.
