# Tropical Fish Spawn Egg

## Obtaining

The Tropical Fish Spawn Egg is available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Tropical Fish Spawn Egg on a block to spawn a Tropical Fish.

## Behavior

Spawn eggs are creative utility items. They do not have durability and are primarily used for testing, mapmaking, creative building, and quickly placing mobs into the world.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:tropical_fish_spawn_egg`.
