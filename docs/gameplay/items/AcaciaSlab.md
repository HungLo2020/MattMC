# Acacia Slab

## Obtaining

Acacia Slabs are crafted from Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Acacia Slabs are half-height building blocks used for floors, roofs, trim, and detail work.

## Behavior

Two slabs can combine into a full block. Slabs can be placed on the upper or lower half of a block space.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_slab` block.
* It appears in the Building Blocks creative tab.
