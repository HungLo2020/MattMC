# Crimson Planks

## Obtaining

Crimson Planks can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Crimson Planks is a core wood-family building block and crafting ingredient.

## Behavior

It behaves like standard plank construction material and can be used in many wood recipes.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:crimson_planks` block.
