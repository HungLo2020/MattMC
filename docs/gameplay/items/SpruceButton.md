# Spruce Button

## Obtaining

Spruce Button can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Spruce Button is a redstone input block. Place it on a surface and press it to emit a short redstone pulse.

## Behavior

Buttons release automatically after a brief delay. Wooden buttons can also be triggered by projectiles.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:spruce_button` block.
