# Banner Pattern (Flow)

## Obtaining

Banner Pattern (Flow) can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal crafting or loot source when available.

## Usage

Banner Pattern (Flow) is used in a loom to apply its pattern to banners.

## Behavior

Banner patterns are reusable pattern items. They unlock a specific banner design when combined with a banner and dye in a loom.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:flow_banner_pattern`.
