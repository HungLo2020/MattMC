# Tuff Brick Wall

## Obtaining

Tuff Brick Wall can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Tuff Brick Wall is used for barriers, posts, edging, and decorative detail.

## Behavior

Walls connect to nearby compatible blocks and change shape based on neighboring blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:tuff_brick_wall` block.
