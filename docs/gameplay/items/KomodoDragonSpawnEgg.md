# Komodo Dragon Spawn Egg

## Obtaining

The Komodo Dragon Spawn Egg is available from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Use the Komodo Dragon Spawn Egg on a block to spawn a Komodo Dragon.

## Behavior

Spawn eggs are creative utility items. They do not have durability and are primarily used for testing, mapmaking, creative building, and quickly placing mobs into the world.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:komodo_dragon_spawn_egg`.
* This mob entry comes from bundled mod content integrated into MattMC.
