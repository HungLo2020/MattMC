# Boiled Emu Egg

Boiled Emu Egg is a food registered as `minecraft:boiled_emu_egg`. It uses the same food properties as Cooked Chicken in this snapshot.

## Food value

Eating one supplies **6 hunger points** (three hunger icons) and **7.2 saturation** before the player's food caps. Its item registration uses the ordinary food setup rather than a dedicated poison or hunger-effect consumable.

## Obtaining limitation

The item is listed in Creative inventory. A cooking or crafting recipe for it was not found in the active recipe data reviewed here. Its name and the existence of a raw [Emu Egg](EmuEgg.md) are not proof of a working boiling or furnace route.

Use it as a registered Creative food or test item until a Survival acquisition route is verified. This is a documentation limit, not a gameplay recipe change.
