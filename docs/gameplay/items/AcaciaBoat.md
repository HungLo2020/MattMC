# Acacia Boat

## Obtaining

Acacia Boats are crafted from five Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from MattMC's JEI-style combined creative menu.

Breaking an Acacia Boat returns it as an item. Tools and weapons that deal more than 4 damage can destroy a boat in one hit.

## Usage

The Acacia Boat is a rideable transport item. Place it on water or land, then interact with it to enter and steer it.

Boats can carry two entities, including players and many mobs. They are useful for moving mobs because most mobs cannot control or leave a boat on their own.

An Acacia Boat can also be used as furnace fuel, lasting 60 seconds and smelting up to 6 items.

## Behavior

The boat moves according to the rider's controls or nearby water currents. It is fastest on water and especially fast on ice variants.

Boats float on still and flowing water. They can sink in downward-flowing water or when pulled down by a magma-block bubble column; magma bubbles also eject passengers.

Riding in a boat does not consume hunger and can prevent fall damage for the boat and its passengers.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:acacia_boat`.
* In MattMC, the creative tab menu has been replaced with a JEI-style combined menu.
* Leads can be attached to boats, which can help move boats that are carrying mobs.
