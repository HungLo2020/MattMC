# Acacia Boat

## Obtaining

Acacia Boats are crafted from Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Acacia Boat is a rideable transport item. Place it on water or land, then interact with it to enter and steer it.

## Behavior

The boat carries players and some mobs. It moves fastest on water and can be broken to return itself as an item.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:acacia_boat`.
* The Acacia Boat appears in the Tools and Utilities creative tab.
