# Tube Coral Block

**Tube Coral Block** (`minecraft:tube_coral_block`) is the blue living full block in the five-species [Coral family](../blocks/Coral.md#tube-coral). [Registration][blocks] [items]

## Obtaining

Use an **unbroken pickaxe with Silk Touch** to recover it alive. A correct pickaxe without Silk Touch gives one Dead Tube Coral Block; the wrong tool gives nothing. The guide covers [Warm Ocean reefs and Wandering Trader offers](../blocks/Coral.md#finding-coral-and-obtaining-more). There is no bundled crafting recipe or Bone Meal full-block growth route. [Loot][loot-tube-coral-block] · [Tool gate][blocks] [pickaxe][] [player-tool][]

## Usage

Keep water-tagged fluid in at least one adjacent face position when placing it. The full block cannot be waterlogged and can turn permanently into its dead form after a dry scheduled check. Read [water and death conditions](../blocks/Coral.md#keeping-living-coral-alive) before building. [Active block behavior][coral-block]
