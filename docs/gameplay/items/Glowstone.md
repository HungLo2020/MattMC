# Glowstone

Glowstone is the item for **`minecraft:glowstone`**, a full block that emits light level **15**. Craft four Glowstone Dust in a 2 × 2 square to make one. [Item][items] · [Light registration][reg-glow] · [Recipe][recipe-glowstone]

Silk Touch recovers one placed block. Otherwise mining gives **2–4 Glowstone Dust** before Fortune, with a final cap of four Dust even with Fortune. A pickaxe is not required by this block's drop gate. See [Glowstone harvesting](../blocks/LuminousBlocks.md#glowstone) for the exact calculation. [Loot][loot-glowstone] · [Registration][reg-glow] · [Player gate][gate]

Nether clusters and the checked Cleric/Wandering Trader offers provide starting sources; use [finding Glowstone](../blocks/LuminousBlocks.md#finding-glowstone). The full block is also used in [Redstone Lamps](../blocks/RedstoneLamp.md) and [Respawn Anchors](../blocks/RespawnAnchor.md); [Glowstone Dust](GlowstoneDust.md) keeps the separate ingredient's uses.
