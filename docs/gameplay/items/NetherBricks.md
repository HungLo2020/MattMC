# Nether Bricks

**Nether Bricks** (`minecraft:nether_bricks`) is the placeable masonry block. The singular [Nether Brick](NetherBrick.md) is its small crafting ingredient. [Block item registration][items]

## Obtaining and use

Craft the block from small Nether Brick items, or collect placed Nether Bricks with an **unbroken pickaxe**. The [block guide](../blocks/NetherBricks.md#obtaining) covers the recipes and checked Fortress route. Correct-tool mining gives the block itself without Silk Touch, rather than returning four small bricks. [Crafting][crafting-nether-bricks] · [Loot][loot-nether-bricks] · [Mining tag][pickaxe]

Place it directly, or follow the [family recipes](../blocks/NetherBricks.md#crafting-and-cracking) for shaped, chiseled or cracked products and the mixed-item fence recipe. The [placed guide](../blocks/NetherBricks.md#placement-and-the-fence) owns construction behavior. [Cracking][smelting-cracked-nether-bricks] · [Chiseled][crafting-chiseled-nether-bricks]
