# Zombie Head

## Obtaining

Zombie Head can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Zombie Head is a decorative head block used for building, display, mapmaking, and themed decoration.

## Behavior

It can be placed as a block and follows the normal placement behavior for head-type blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:zombie_head` block.
