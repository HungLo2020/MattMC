# Acacia Trapdoor

## Obtaining

Acacia Trapdoors are crafted from Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Acacia Trapdoor is used as a hatch, shutter, decorative panel, or redstone-controlled opening.

## Behavior

It can be opened by hand or redstone. As a wooden trapdoor, it is flammable and can be used for compact decorative detailing.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_trapdoor` block.
* It appears in the Building Blocks creative tab.
