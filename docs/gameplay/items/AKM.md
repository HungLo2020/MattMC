# AKM

From jungles to bays, it's always present.

## Obtaining

AKM can be obtained from the Creative Menu. It is registered as `minecraft:ak47`.

## Usage

AKM is a TaCZ firearm. It uses [7.62x39mm Bullet](762x39mmBullet.md) and fires with the TaCZ gun controls. Reload it with the reload key and aim down sights with the aim key.

## Damage Falloff

* 30 blocks: 9
* 60 blocks: 7.5
* infinite blocks: 6
