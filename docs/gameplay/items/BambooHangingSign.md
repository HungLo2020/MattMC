# Bamboo Hanging Sign

## Obtaining

Bamboo Hanging Sign can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Bamboo Hanging Sign is used to display editable text in the world.

## Behavior

It keeps its written text after placement and can be dyed or waxed to control editing.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:bamboo_hanging_sign` block.
