# Iron Ingot

Iron Ingot (`minecraft:iron_ingot`) is a processed metal used in tools, equipment, workstations, and storage.

## Obtaining

Smelt or blast **Raw Iron, Iron Ore, or Deepslate Iron Ore** to produce one ingot per input. Furnace recipes take 200 ticks; Blast Furnace recipes take 100. [Raw Iron](RawIron.md) is the ordinary correct-tool mining drop, while Silk Touch collects the ore block.

One Block of Iron unpacks into nine ingots. Nine Iron Nuggets in a 3 × 3 square make one ingot. These are selected verified acquisition routes, not a full mob-drop or chest-loot catalog.

## Useful crafting choices

- **Iron Pickaxe:** three ingots across the top above two centered Sticks
- **Bucket:** two ingots across an upper row's outer slots with one centered below
- **Shears:** two ingots diagonally opposite each other
- **Shield:** one ingot in the upper center, six wooden-tool-material-tag items around/below it in the bundled shield pattern
- **Anvil:** three Iron Blocks plus four ingots, equivalent to 31 ingots when crafting the blocks yourself

The Iron tool-material repair tag contains Iron Ingot. [Anvil mechanics](../mechanics/AnvilMechanics.md) covers how much each material repairs and the associated cost. Iron is also a valid material for the matching armor repair tags, rather than a universal repair ingredient for every metallic-looking item.

Nine ingots pack into one Block of Iron and unpack back into nine. Keep the difference between Iron Ingots, Raw Iron, and Iron Nuggets when following recipes.
