# Crimson Hyphae

## Obtaining

Crimson Hyphae can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Crimson Hyphae is a wood-family building block and crafting material.

## Behavior

It follows normal wood or fungus-stem behavior. Axe interactions may strip compatible blocks into their stripped form.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:crimson_hyphae` block.
