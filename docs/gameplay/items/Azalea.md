# Azalea

## Obtaining

Azalea can be found in lush cave-related generation and can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Azalea is used as a decorative plant and can be grown into an azalea tree.

## Behavior

It can be placed on valid plant-supporting blocks. Bone Meal can be used to grow it when conditions are valid.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:azalea` block.
* It appears in the Natural Blocks creative tab.
