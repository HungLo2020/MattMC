# Banner Pattern (Piglin)

## Obtaining

Banner Pattern (Piglin) can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal crafting or loot source when available.

## Usage

Banner Pattern (Piglin) is used in a loom to apply its pattern to banners.

## Behavior

Banner patterns are reusable pattern items. They unlock a specific banner design when combined with a banner and dye in a loom.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:piglin_banner_pattern`.
