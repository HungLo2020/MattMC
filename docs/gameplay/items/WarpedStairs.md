# Warped Stairs

## Obtaining

Warped Stairs can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Warped Stairs is a warped wood or Nether vegetation block item used for building and decoration.

## Behavior

When placed, it creates the `minecraft:warped_stairs` block and follows the normal placement rules for that warped block type.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:warped_stairs` block.
