# Anvil

## Obtaining

Anvils are crafted from Iron Blocks and Iron Ingots. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Anvil is used to repair items, rename items, and combine enchantments.

## Behavior

Anvils can fall like gravity blocks and can become damaged through use or falling impacts.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:anvil` block.
* Chipped and Damaged Anvils are separate registered block items.
