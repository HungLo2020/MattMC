# Limestone Pillar

## Obtaining

Limestone Pillar can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Limestone Pillar is a limestone-family building block integrated into MattMC.

## Behavior

It behaves as a stone-like building block with its configured mining and placement rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:limestone_pillar` block.
* This block comes from bundled cave content integrated into MattMC.
