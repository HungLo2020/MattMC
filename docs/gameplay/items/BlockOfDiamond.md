# Block of Diamond

## Obtaining

Block of Diamond can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Block of Diamond is placed in the world as a block and used for building, decoration, utility, or environmental detail.

## Behavior

When placed, it behaves as the corresponding block. Breaking the block returns drops according to the block's normal loot rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:diamond_block` block.
