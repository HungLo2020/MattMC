# Obsidian

**Obsidian** is the item form of `minecraft:obsidian`, used for ordinary Nether portal frames and several utility-block recipes. It is separate from [Crying Obsidian](CryingObsidian.md). [Item registration][items]

## Obtaining

Collect a placed block with an **unbroken Diamond or Netherite Pickaxe**, or obtain the item through the checked [Piglin barter and ruined-portal routes](../blocks/Obsidian.md#finding-both-forms). See the block guide for [making Obsidian from Lava and Water](../blocks/Obsidian.md#making-ordinary-obsidian) and exact [mining/drop rules](../blocks/Obsidian.md#mining-and-block-properties). There is no bundled recipe producing ordinary Obsidian. [Mining loot][loot] · [Recipe loading][recipes]

## Uses

Place it for building or a [Nether portal frame](../dimensions/Nether.md#building-and-using-a-portal). It is also an ingredient for an [Enchanting Table](../blocks/EnchantingTable.md), [Beacon](../blocks/Beacon.md), and [Ender Chest](EnderChest.md). It cannot replace Crying Obsidian in the [Respawn Anchor recipe](../blocks/RespawnAnchor.md#crafting-and-recovering-an-anchor). The [block guide](../blocks/Obsidian.md#obsidian) owns placed behavior and the distinction between the two forms.
