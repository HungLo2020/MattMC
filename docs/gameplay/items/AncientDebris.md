# Ancient Debris

## Obtaining

Ancient Debris is mined in the Nether and can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Ancient Debris is smelted into Netherite Scrap and is also used as a durable decorative block.

## Behavior

The block is highly blast resistant, requires the correct tool for drops, and its item form is fire resistant.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:ancient_debris` block.
* It appears in Natural Blocks and Ingredients.
