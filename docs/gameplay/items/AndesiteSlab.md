# Andesite Slab

## Obtaining

Andesite Slabs are crafted from Andesite or obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Andesite Slabs are half-height stone blocks used for paths, floors, roofs, and stone detailing.

## Behavior

Two slabs can combine into a full block. Slabs can be placed on the upper or lower half of a block space.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:andesite_slab` block.
* It appears in the Building Blocks creative tab.
