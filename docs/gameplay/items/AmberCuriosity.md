# Amber Curiosity

## Obtaining

Amber Curiosity can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from any Alex's Caves progression sources enabled in the current build.

## Usage

Amber Curiosity is a special item associated with Alex's Caves content.

## Behavior

It is registered as a normal item and appears with other ingredient-style items in the Creative Menu.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:amber_curiosity`.
* It appears in the Ingredients creative tab.
