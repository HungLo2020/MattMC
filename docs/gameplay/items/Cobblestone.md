# Cobblestone

**Cobblestone** (`minecraft:cobblestone`) is a placeable building block and an early crafting material. [Item registration][items]

## Obtaining and use

Mine ordinary Stone with an **unbroken pickaxe without Silk Touch**, or mine an existing Cobblestone block with a pickaxe. Silk Touch on Cobblestone still gives Cobblestone. The [Stone family guide](../blocks/Stone.md#obtaining) also explains the checked water/lava conversion. [Stone loot][loot-stone] · [Cobblestone loot][loot-cobblestone] · [Mining tag][pickaxe]

Use it directly for building, smelt it into Stone, or turn it into mossy blocks, stairs, slabs or walls using the [canonical family recipes](../blocks/Stone.md#uses). It is accepted by the [Furnace](../blocks/Furnace.md#crafting-and-mining) and [Stone Pickaxe](StonePickaxe.md) material tags. Do not replace those ingredients with ordinary Stone merely because the names are similar. [Smelting][smelting-stone] · [Material tags][stone-materials] [tool-materials]
