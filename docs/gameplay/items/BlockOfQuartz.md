# Block of Quartz

**Block of Quartz** (`minecraft:quartz_block`) is the starting block for quartz construction, distinct from the small Nether Quartz ingredient. [Registration][items]

## Obtaining and use

Craft it from Nether Quartz using the [canonical construction recipe](../blocks/Quartz.md#crafting-and-smoothing). A placed block drops itself with an **unbroken pickaxe**, including Wood, without Silk Touch. It does not unpack into four Nether Quartz items. [Recipe][crafting-quartz-block] · [Loot][loot-quartz-block] · [Mining tag][pickaxe]

Use it directly for building, or follow the [Quartz guide](../blocks/Quartz.md) for pillars, chiseled blocks, bricks, smoothing, stairs and slabs. The [stonecutting table](../blocks/Quartz.md#stonecutting) gives exact yields. Smooth Quartz and Quartz Bricks are different items with different accepted recipes. [Pillar recipe][crafting-quartz-pillar] · [Brick recipe][crafting-quartz-bricks] · [Smoothing][smelting-smooth-quartz]

Related: [Nether Quartz](NetherQuartz.md) · [Smooth Quartz Block](SmoothQuartzBlock.md) · [Items](Items.md)
