# Ochre Froglight

## Obtaining

Ochre Froglight can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Ochre Froglight is a decorative light-emitting block.

## Behavior

It emits light and behaves as a solid decorative block.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:ochre_froglight` block.
