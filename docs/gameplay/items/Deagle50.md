# Deagle 50

It can destroy anything just like your wrists.

## Obtaining

Deagle 50 can be obtained from the Creative Menu. It is registered as `minecraft:deagle`.

## Usage

Deagle 50 is a TaCZ firearm. It uses [.50 AE](50AE.md) and fires with the TaCZ gun controls. Reload it with the reload key and aim down sights with the aim key.

* 15 blocks: 16
* 30 blocks: 14
* 50 blocks: 12
* infinite blocks: 10
