# Pumpkin

**Pumpkin** (`minecraft:pumpkin`) is a placeable fruit block used for seeds, carving, and food crafting. The Pumpkin itself is not directly edible. [Registration][item]

## Obtaining

Harvest a wild Pumpkin or a fruit grown by a Pumpkin stem. An ordinary block harvest gives one Pumpkin. See [Pumpkin and Melon farming](../blocks/PumpkinAndMelon.md) for natural sources, stem growth, drops, and harvesting without replanting.

## Uses

- Craft [Pumpkin Seeds](PumpkinSeeds.md#crafting) to start or expand a farm
- Use it in the [Pumpkin Pie recipe](Egg.md#selected-recipes), alongside Sugar and an accepted Egg
- Place it and use Shears for [carving and seed collection](../blocks/PumpkinAndMelon.md#carving-a-pumpkin)

Carved Pumpkin is a different block and is not the Pumpkin ingredient named in those seed and pie recipes. [Seed recipe][seeds] · [Pie recipe][pie]
