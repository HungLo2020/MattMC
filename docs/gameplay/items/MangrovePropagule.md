# Mangrove Propagule

## Obtaining

Mangrove Propagule can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Mangrove Propagule is planted to grow mangrove trees and can also be used as natural decoration.

## Behavior

It can grow when planted in valid conditions and follows normal mangrove propagule behavior.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:mangrove_propagule` block.
