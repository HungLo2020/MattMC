# End Stone Brick Wall

## Obtaining

End Stone Brick Wall can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

End Stone Brick Wall is used for barriers, posts, edging, and decorative stone detail.

## Behavior

Walls connect to nearby compatible blocks and change shape based on neighboring blocks.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:end_stone_brick_wall` block.
