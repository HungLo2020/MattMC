# Acacia Door

## Obtaining

Acacia Doors are crafted from Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from MattMC's JEI-style combined creative menu.

As an Overworld wooden door, an Acacia Door can also be used as furnace fuel and smelts 1 item.

## Usage

The Acacia Door is placed as a two-block-tall doorway. Interact with it or power it with redstone to open and close it.

Doors are switchable barriers for players, mobs, boats, items, and minecarts. They can be used as normal entrances, redstone-controlled gates, or compact movement blockers.

## Behavior

The door has upper and lower block halves. It must be placed on a full solid supporting block, with the upper half occupying the block above the placement position.

It can be opened by hand, redstone signals, villagers, and other normal wooden-door interactions. As a wooden door, it can be broken by zombies and vindicators in Hard difficulty under the normal door-breaking rules.

When placed next to a matching door, the hinge logic can create a double-door layout with handles together and hinges on opposite sides.

Doors placed underwater create a breathable air pocket because they are not waterlogged.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_door` block.
* In MattMC, the creative tab menu has been replaced with a JEI-style combined menu.
