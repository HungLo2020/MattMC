# Acacia Door

## Obtaining

Acacia Doors are crafted from Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Acacia Door is placed as a two-block-tall doorway. Interact with it or power it with redstone to open and close it.

## Behavior

The door has upper and lower block halves. It can be opened by hand, redstone signals, villagers, and other normal wooden-door interactions.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_door` block.
* It appears with other acacia building blocks in the Building Blocks creative tab.
