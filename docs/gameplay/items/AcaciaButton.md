# Acacia Button

## Obtaining

Acacia Buttons are crafted from Acacia Planks. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

The Acacia Button is a redstone input block. Place it on a surface and press it to emit a short redstone pulse.

## Behavior

The button stays pressed briefly, then releases automatically. As a wooden button, it can also be triggered by arrows and other projectiles.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_button` block.
* It appears with other acacia building blocks in the Building Blocks creative tab.
