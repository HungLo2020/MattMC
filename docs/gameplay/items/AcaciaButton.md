# Acacia Button

## Obtaining

Acacia Buttons are crafted from one Acacia Plank. They can also be obtained from MattMC's JEI-style combined creative menu.

## Usage

The Acacia Button is a redstone input block. Place it on a surface and press it to emit a short redstone pulse.

Buttons can be attached to the top, side, or bottom of a full opaque block.

## Behavior

The button stays pressed briefly, then releases automatically. As a wooden button, it remains active for 30 game ticks, or 1.5 seconds.

While active, it emits redstone power level 15, powers adjacent redstone dust and components, and strongly powers the block it is attached to.

As a wooden button, it can also be activated by projectiles such as arrows. A projectile-triggered wooden button can remain active until the projectile is removed or despawns.

The button drops as an item if its supporting block is removed, if a piston tries to move it, or if it otherwise loses valid placement support.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_button` block.
* In MattMC, the creative tab menu has been replaced with a JEI-style combined menu.
