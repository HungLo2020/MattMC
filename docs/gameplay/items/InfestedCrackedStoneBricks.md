# Infested Cracked Stone Bricks

## Obtaining

Infested Cracked Stone Bricks can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Infested Cracked Stone Bricks is a hidden hostile variant of a stone block.

## Behavior

When broken or disturbed, infested blocks can release silverfish instead of behaving like a normal stone block.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:infested_cracked_stone_bricks` block.
