# Cut Sandstone Slab

A **Cut Sandstone Slab** (`minecraft:cut_sandstone_slab`) places the half-height Cut Sandstone building form. It is a separate item from ordinary and Smooth Sandstone Slabs, and it cannot combine with either of them into a double slab.

## Obtaining

Craft **3 Cut Sandstone in one horizontal row into 6 Cut Sandstone Slabs**. Alternatively, stonecut **1 ordinary Sandstone or 1 Cut Sandstone into 2 Cut Sandstone Slabs**. The ordinary Sandstone shortcut avoids crafting Cut blocks first. [Crafting recipe][craft] · [Sandstone cutting][base-cut] · [Cut Sandstone cutting][cut]

Use an unbroken pickaxe, including Wood, to collect the placed slab. A single slab drops one item and a double slab drops two; Silk Touch is unnecessary and Fortune adds no multiplier. Hand mining does not meet the correct-tool requirement. [Exact loot][loot] · [Tool rules](../blocks/Sandstone.md#obtaining-and-mining)

## Placement

Choose a top or bottom half by the clicked face and height, or place a second identical slab into the empty half to make a double slab. Single slabs can be waterlogged; combining into a double slab clears that state. The placed form remains when support is removed. [Shared slab controls](../blocks/Stone.md#placing-shaped-blocks)

Its **hardness 2 and blast resistance 6** differ from the Cut Sandstone full block's 0.8/0.8 properties. Read the [Sandstone family guide](../blocks/Sandstone.md) for all twenty forms and their recipes. [Registration][block]
