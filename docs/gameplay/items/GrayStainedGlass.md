# Gray Stained Glass

## Obtaining

Gray Stained Glass can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Gray Stained Glass is a transparent building or crafting block used for windows, bottles, panes, and decoration.

## Behavior

Glass blocks are fragile transparent materials and follow their normal mining and drop rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:gray_stained_glass` block.
