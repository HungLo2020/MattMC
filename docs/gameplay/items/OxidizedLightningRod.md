# Oxidized Lightning Rod

## Obtaining

Oxidized Lightning Rod can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Oxidized Lightning Rod is a fully oxidized copper-family block used for building, decoration, redstone, or utility depending on the variant.

## Behavior

Oxidized copper blocks represent the final oxidation state and follow their normal mining, placement, and interaction rules.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:oxidized_lightning_rod` block.
