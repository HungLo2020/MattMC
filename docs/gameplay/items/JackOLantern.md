# Jack o'Lantern

## Obtaining

Jack o'Lantern can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Jack o'Lantern is a carved pumpkin light block used for decoration and seasonal builds.

## Behavior

It emits light and can be placed as a directional decorative block.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:jack_o_lantern` block.
