# Block of Bamboo

**Block of Bamboo** is the item form of `minecraft:bamboo_block`. The [block guide](../blocks/BambooBlocks.md#block-of-bamboo) owns its placed behavior. [Item registration][item]

## Obtaining

Make it with the [Bamboo packing recipe](../blocks/Bamboo.md#selected-uses-and-fuel), or collect a placed block. Hand mining works; an axe is faster.

## Usage

Use it for axis-oriented building, the [Bamboo Planks conversion](../blocks/WoodConstruction.md#planks-and-materials), or the [stripping route](../blocks/BambooBlocks.md#block-of-stripped-bamboo).

## Behavior

It drops one matching block in ordinary mining and supplies 300 default Furnace fuel ticks. Placement, stripping, fire, and fuel details belong to [Bamboo blocks](../blocks/BambooBlocks.md).
