# Carmine Froglight

**`minecraft:carmine_froglight`** is a registered full building block. Its item appears in Creative, emits light level **15** when placed, and uses the same face-selected pillar axis as the other Froglights. [Registration][reg-frogs] · [Item][items] · [Creative entry][creative] · [Axis placement][pillar]

A placed Carmine Froglight drops **one matching item**, including by hand; Silk Touch and Fortune are unnecessary. That recovery rule does not establish a starting Survival source. [Loot][loot-carmine_froglight] · [Harvest gate][gate]

**Survival acquisition is unverified in the checked source.** The Magma Cube loot has only the Temperate/Ochre, Cold/Verdant, and Warm/Pearlescent mappings, with no Carmine result. No Carmine crafting recipe or production route was found in the bounded source/resource audit. Do not assume that Rain Frogs supply it. [Magma Cube loot][entity-loot-magma_cube]

See [Carmine Froglight](../blocks/LuminousBlocks.md#carmine-froglight) for the audit limits and [all Froglights](../blocks/LuminousBlocks.md#froglights) for shared placement and recovery.
