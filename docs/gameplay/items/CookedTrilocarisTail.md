# Cooked Trilocaris Tail

## Obtaining

Cooked Trilocaris Tail can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Cooked Trilocaris Tail is a food item.

## Behavior

Eating it restores hunger and saturation according to its food values.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:cooked_trilocaris_tail`.
