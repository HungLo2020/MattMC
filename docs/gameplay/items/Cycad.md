# Cycad

Cycad is the block item `minecraft:cycad`. A stackable plant whose top can grow with bone meal while the connected column is shorter than four blocks. The success check is 50%, and space above must be replaceable.

## Obtaining

The item is explicitly listed in Creative. A naturally generated starter source or crafting route was not established in the checked bundled data.

Placed Fiddleheads and Cycads have ordinary self-drop loot, subject to explosion survival. Once a starter plant is available, see the verified bone-meal propagation rules.

## Use

Place the item for decoration and propagation. The [Primordial plant guide](../blocks/PrimordialPlants.md) explains its support requirements, growth limits, and harvesting behavior. Do not assume an upstream habitat or food use from the item's name.
