# Cycad

## Obtaining

Cycad can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Cycad is used as vegetation, natural decoration, or plant-related building detail.

## Behavior

It can be placed on valid supporting blocks and follows the normal placement and growth behavior for its plant type.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:cycad` block.
