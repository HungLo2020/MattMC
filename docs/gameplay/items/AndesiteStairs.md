# Andesite Stairs

## Obtaining

Andesite Stairs are crafted from Andesite or obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Andesite Stairs are used for stone stairways, rooflines, trim, and angled details.

## Behavior

Stairs connect into corner shapes depending on nearby stair blocks and placement direction.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:andesite_stairs` block.
* It appears in the Building Blocks creative tab.
