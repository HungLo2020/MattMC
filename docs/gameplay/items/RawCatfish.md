# Raw Catfish

## Obtaining

Raw Catfish can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Raw Catfish is a raw food or raw resource item.

## Behavior

Raw foods can often be cooked, while raw resources can be smelted or crafted according to their recipes.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:raw_catfish`.
* This item comes from bundled mod content integrated into MattMC.
