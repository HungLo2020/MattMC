# Music Disc (Far)

## Obtaining

Music Disc (Far) can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal loot source when available.

## Usage

Music Disc (Far) is played in a Jukebox.

## Behavior

Music discs are reusable media items. Insert one into a Jukebox to play its track, then remove it to stop or recover it.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:music_disc_far`.
