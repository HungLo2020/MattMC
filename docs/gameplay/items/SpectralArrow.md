# Spectral Arrow

## Obtaining

Spectral Arrow can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Spectral Arrow is ammunition for bows, crossbows, dispensers, and other arrow-firing mechanics.

## Behavior

Fired arrows can damage entities and follow their configured projectile behavior.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:spectral_arrow`.
