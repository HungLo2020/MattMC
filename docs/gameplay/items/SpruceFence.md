# Spruce Fence

## Obtaining

Spruce Fence can be obtained from the Creative Menu and by collecting or crafting its block where applicable.

## Usage

Spruce Fence is used for barriers, railings, animal pens, and decorative trim.

## Behavior

Fence blocks connect to nearby compatible blocks and have taller collision than their visual height.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:spruce_fence` block.
