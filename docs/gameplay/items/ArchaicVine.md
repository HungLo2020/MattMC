# Archaic Vine

## Obtaining

Archaic Vine can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from any Alex's Caves generation sources enabled in the current build.

## Usage

Archaic Vine is used as hanging prehistoric vegetation for cave and ancient-biome builds.

## Behavior

It grows as a vine with a head and plant body block, similar to other hanging plant systems.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:archaic_vine` block.
* It appears in the Natural Blocks creative tab.
