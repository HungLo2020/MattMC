# Bucket of Small Catfish

## Obtaining

Bucket of Small Catfish is obtained by using a Water Bucket on a Small Catfish, or from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Bucket of Small Catfish is used to carry and release Small Catfish.

## Behavior

Using the bucket places the contained creature into water and returns an empty Bucket.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:small_catfish_bucket`.
