# Tall Grass

**Tall Grass** (`minecraft:tall_grass`) is the matching item for the [placed Tall Grass form](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#tall-grass). [Tall items] · [Display names]

## Obtaining

The **Tall Grass inventory item** can occur in Savanna village-house chests. Shearing the planted tall form yields **two Short Grass**, not this item. See [the checked acquisition routes](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#finding-plants-and-tall-inventory-items).

## Usage

Use one item to place a two-block plant, or compost it. A placed Tall Grass plant can also be produced from Short Grass through [Bone Meal](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#bone-meal-and-a-renewable-supply).

## Behavior

Its [two-half support and harvesting rules](../blocks/GrassAndFerns.md#planting-and-tall-halves) differ from [Tall Dry Grass](../blocks/ShrubsAndDryGrass.md#tall-dry-grass), which occupies one block.
