# Cut Red Sandstone

**Cut Red Sandstone** (`minecraft:cut_red_sandstone`) places the Cut full-block finish in the red Sandstone family.

## Obtaining

Craft **4 ordinary [Red Sandstone](RedSandstone.md) in a 2 × 2 square into 4 Cut Red Sandstone**, or stonecut **1 ordinary Red Sandstone into 1 Cut Red Sandstone**. Both routes preserve the number of full blocks. [Crafting][recipe-1] · [Stonecutting][recipe-2]

Mine with an **unbroken pickaxe, including Wood**. In ordinary mining, the placed block drops **1 Cut Red Sandstone item**. Hand mining does not collect the normal drop. Silk Touch is unnecessary and Fortune adds no multiplier. [Exact loot][loot] · [Family mining rules](../blocks/Sandstone.md#obtaining-and-mining)

## Usage

Use it for full-block building detail or make [Cut Red Sandstone Slab](CutRedSandstoneSlab.md)s. It also substitutes for ordinary Red Sandstone in the crafting recipe for [Red Sandstone Stairs](RedSandstoneStairs.md), including mixtures with other accepted finishes of this color. That stair substitution applies to crafting; the Stonecutter uses ordinary Red Sandstone for those stairs. See the [family recipe table](../blocks/Sandstone.md#crafting-finishes-and-shapes).

## Behavior

The full block has no selectable facing or pillar axis and stays in place when support underneath is removed. Mining it with the correct tool keeps the Cut finish. See [Sandstone placement and use](../blocks/Sandstone.md#placement-and-use).

## Notes

This item places the `minecraft:cut_red_sandstone` block. The [Sandstone family guide](../blocks/Sandstone.md#registered-forms-and-loot) lists its related forms. [Item registration][item] · [Block registration][block]
