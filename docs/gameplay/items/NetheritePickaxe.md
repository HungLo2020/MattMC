# Netherite Pickaxe

## Obtaining

Netherite Pickaxe can be obtained from the Creative Menu and from its normal survival sources when those sources are available.

## Usage

Netherite Pickaxe is a netherite-tier tool or weapon used for its matching action.

## Behavior

It has durability, is fire resistant as a netherite item, and follows normal tool or weapon behavior.

## Notes

* This item is registered as `minecraft:netherite_pickaxe`.
