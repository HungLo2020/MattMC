# Acacia Planks

## Obtaining

Acacia Planks are crafted from Acacia Logs, Acacia Wood, Stripped Acacia Logs, or Stripped Acacia Wood. They can also be obtained from the Creative Menu.

## Usage

Acacia Planks are a core orange-toned wood building block and crafting ingredient.

## Behavior

They behave like standard wooden planks. They are flammable and can be used in many wood recipes.

## Notes

* This item is the item form of the `minecraft:acacia_planks` block.
* It appears in the Building Blocks creative tab.
