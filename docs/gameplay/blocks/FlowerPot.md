# Flower Pot

A **Flower Pot** (`minecraft:flower_pot`) displays one supported plant. Craft an empty pot, place it, then add a plant; you can recover the plant while leaving the pot in place. MattMC registers **40 filled forms**, including Pewen Sapling, Ancient Sapling, and Pewen Pines. [Pot registration][register] · [Item registration][item] · [Potting interaction][use]

## Crafting

At a [Crafting Table](CraftingTable.md), arrange **three loose [Bricks](../items/Brick.md)** in a V: one in each outer slot of the upper row and one in the center slot of the row below. The result is **one Flower Pot**. The recipe uses `minecraft:brick`, not the full `minecraft:bricks` building block. Use the [Clay and Bricks guide](ClayAndBricks.md#crafting-and-smelting) for making that ingredient. [Exact recipe][recipe]

## Adding and removing plants

1. Place an empty Flower Pot.
2. Use one of the supported plant items below on it. In Survival, one plant is consumed and the pot becomes that plant's filled form.
3. To recover the plant, use the filled pot with an **empty main hand**. It goes into your inventory, or drops beside you if it cannot be added. The empty pot remains.
4. Add the next plant to the empty pot. Holding another supported plant against a filled pot does not swap the contents directly.

An unsupported main-hand item can also reach the removal interaction; the empty-hand method is the clearest way to request it. These are ordinary block-use interactions, so secondary use can bypass them when you are holding an item. [Potting and removal][use] · [Active use dispatch][dispatch]

[Dead Bush](DeadBush.md#placement-and-decoration) follows these same potting rules. Its potted loot returns the bush intact, so collecting the filled display does not use the unpotted bush's Stick alternative. [Potted Dead Bush loot][dead-bush-loot]

## Placement and support

**The checked MattMC source does not require a supporting block underneath a Flower Pot.** The pot inherits an always-true survival check, so removing the block below does not make the pot break through its downward-neighbor callback. Ordinary item placement still needs a placeable, unobstructed destination. This applies to empty and filled forms. [Pot class and neighbor callback][pot] · [Inherited survival][support] · [Item placement checks][placement]

Pots break instantly and have no correct-tool requirement. Their registered piston reaction is **destroy**, rather than ordinary block movement. See [Pistons](Pistons.md#what-can-move) for the shared movement limits and destruction handling. [Pot properties][properties]

| Empty Flower Pot | **1 Flower Pot** |
| Any filled Flower Pot | **1 Flower Pot + 1 matching plant item** |

Silk Touch does not keep a filled pot as one inventory item, and Fortune does not multiply these drops. Shears are not required to recover a potted Dead Bush or other potted plant. [Empty-pot loot][empty-loot] · [Filled-pot example][dead-bush-loot] · [All filled loot tables](#supported-plants) · [No-tool drop gate][gate] · [Mining dispatch][break]

Explosion recovery differs from ordinary mining. The empty pot and almost all filled-pot pools use explosion-survival checks. **Potted Pewen Sapling and Potted Ancient Sapling are exceptions:** their pot-item pool lacks that check, while their plant-item pool still has it. Do not assume the pot and plant always survive a blast together. [Pewen Sapling loot][pewen-loot] · [Ancient Sapling loot][ancient-loot] · [Usual filled-pot loot][dead-bush-loot]

## Growth and Eyeblossoms

Potted saplings do not grow into trees, and Bone Meal does not grow or duplicate the displayed plants through the pot. The pot class has no Bone Meal target and only its two Eyeblossom forms receive plant-specific random ticking. [Pot behavior][pot] · [Bone Meal dispatch][bone-meal]

In **natural dimensions**, a potted Eyeblossom changes to the **open form during day-time ticks 12,600–23,400 inclusive**, and the **closed form otherwise**, when its random tick runs. This uses the dimension's time; it does not test whether the pot can see the sky or moon. The two forms retain their current state outside natural dimensions. Removal and mining recover whichever open/closed plant the pot currently contains.
