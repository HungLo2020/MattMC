# Salmon

The **Salmon** is a passive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Salmon can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Salmon Spawn Egg](../items/SalmonSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Salmon is classified as passive here: it does not normally start fights with players. Any special interactions, taming, breeding, bucket, mount, or utility behavior still follows its in-game implementation.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:salmon`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:salmon_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.WATER_AMBIENT`.
* Its entity class is `Salmon`.
* Its registered size is `0.7, 0.4` blocks.
