# Endermite

The **Endermite** is an aggressive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Endermite can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Endermite Spawn Egg](../items/EndermiteSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Endermite is classified as aggressive here: it is hostile, uses hostile targeting, or has player-targeting behavior in its implementation.
