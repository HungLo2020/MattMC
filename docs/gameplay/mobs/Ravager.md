# Ravager

The **Ravager** is a boss or mini-boss in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Ravager can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Ravager Spawn Egg](../items/RavagerSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Ravager is listed with bosses and mini-bosses because it is a major hostile encounter, raid-scale threat, or boss-style entity rather than a normal passive, neutral, or aggressive mob.
