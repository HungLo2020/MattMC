# Glow Squid

The **Glow Squid** is a passive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Glow Squid can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Glow Squid Spawn Egg](../items/GlowSquidSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Glow Squid is classified as passive here: it does not normally start fights with players. Any special interactions, taming, breeding, bucket, mount, or utility behavior still follows its in-game implementation.
