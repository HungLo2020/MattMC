# Comb Jelly

The **Comb Jelly** is a passive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Comb Jelly can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Comb Jelly Spawn Egg](../items/CombJellySpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Comb Jelly is classified as passive here: it does not normally start fights with players. Any special interactions, taming, breeding, bucket, mount, or utility behavior still follows its in-game implementation.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:comb_jelly`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:comb_jelly_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.WATER_AMBIENT`.
* Its entity class is `EntityCombJelly`.
* Its registered size is `0.75, 0.75` blocks.
* This mob comes from bundled Alex's Mobs content integrated into MattMC.
