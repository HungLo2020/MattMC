# Platypus

The **Platypus** is a passive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Platypus can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Platypus Spawn Egg](../items/PlatypusSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Platypus is classified as passive here: it does not normally start fights with players. Any special interactions, taming, breeding, bucket, mount, or utility behavior still follows its in-game implementation.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:platypus`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:platypus_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.CREATURE`.
* Its entity class is `EntityPlatypus`.
* Its registered size is `0.8, 0.5` blocks.
* This mob comes from bundled Alex's Mobs content integrated into MattMC.
