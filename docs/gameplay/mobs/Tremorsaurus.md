# Tremorsaurus

The **Tremorsaurus** is an aggressive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Tremorsaurus can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Tremorsaurus Spawn Egg](../items/TremorsaurusSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Tremorsaurus is classified as aggressive here: it is hostile, uses hostile targeting, or has player-targeting behavior in its implementation.
