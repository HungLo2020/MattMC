# Bogged

The **Bogged** is an aggressive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Bogged can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Bogged Spawn Egg](../items/BoggedSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Bogged is classified as aggressive here: it is hostile, uses hostile targeting, or has player-targeting behavior in its implementation.

* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.MONSTER`.
