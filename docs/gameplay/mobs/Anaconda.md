# Anaconda

The **Anaconda** is an aggressive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Anaconda can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Anaconda Spawn Egg](../items/AnacondaSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Anaconda is classified as aggressive here: it is hostile, uses hostile targeting, or has player-targeting behavior in its implementation.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:anaconda`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:anaconda_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.CREATURE`.
* Its entity class is `EntityAnaconda`.
* Its registered size is `1.2, 0.6` blocks.
* This mob comes from bundled Alex's Mobs content integrated into MattMC.
