# Chicken

The **Chicken** is a passive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Chicken can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Chicken Spawn Egg](../items/ChickenSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Chicken is classified as passive here: it does not normally start fights with players. Any special interactions, taming, breeding, bucket, mount, or utility behavior still follows its in-game implementation.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:chicken`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:chicken_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.CREATURE`.
* Its entity class is `Chicken`.
* Its registered size is `0.4, 0.7` blocks.
* This mob comes from the base MattMC/Minecraft entity set.
