# Wither

The **Wither** is a boss or mini-boss in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Wither can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Wither Spawn Egg](../items/WitherSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Wither is listed with bosses and mini-bosses because it is a major hostile encounter, raid-scale threat, or boss-style entity rather than a normal passive, neutral, or aggressive mob.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:wither`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:wither_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.MONSTER`.
* Its entity class is `WitherBoss`.
* Its registered size is `0.9, 3.5` blocks.
* This mob comes from the base MattMC/Minecraft entity set.
