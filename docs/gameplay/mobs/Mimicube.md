# Mimicube

The **Mimicube** is an aggressive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Mimicube can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Mimicube Spawn Egg](../items/MimicubeSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Mimicube is classified as aggressive here: it is hostile, uses hostile targeting, or has player-targeting behavior in its implementation.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:mimicube`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:mimicube_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.MONSTER`.
* Its entity class is `EntityMimicube`.
* Its registered size is `1.02, 1.02` blocks.
* This mob comes from bundled Alex's Mobs content integrated into MattMC.
