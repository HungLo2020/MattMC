# Spectre

The **Spectre** is a passive mob in MattMC.

## Obtaining

Spectre can be encountered through its normal spawning rules, structures, events, or content-specific systems when those systems are available. For creative testing and mapmaking, use the [Spectre Spawn Egg](../items/SpectreSpawnEgg.md).

## Behavior

Spectre is classified as passive here: it does not normally start fights with players. Any special interactions, taming, breeding, bucket, mount, or utility behavior still follows its in-game implementation.

## Notes

* This mob is registered as `minecraft:spectre`.
* Its spawn egg is registered as `minecraft:spectre_spawn_egg`.
* Its entity registration uses `MobCategory.MONSTER`.
* Its entity class is `EntitySpectre`.
* Its registered size is `1.0, 1.5` blocks.
* This mob comes from bundled Alex's Mobs content integrated into MattMC.
