# Giant Squid

The **Giant Squid** is a large aquatic mob from the bundled Alex's Mobs content. Use a [Giant Squid Spawn Egg](../items/GiantSquidSpawnEgg.md) for Creative aquariums or encounters. It hunts guardians, can retaliate against attackers, and avoids [Cachalot Whales](CachalotWhale.md). [Goals](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L190-L207)

## Where to find it

The entity is actively registered as `minecraft:giant_squid` in the water-creature category, with a registered body size of **2.5 blocks wide × 1.5 blocks tall**. Its attributes and renderer are also registered. [Entity registration](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/EntityType.java#L697-L703) · [Attributes](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/ai/attributes/DefaultAttributes.java#L170-L177) · [Renderer registration](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/client/renderer/entity/EntityRenderers.java#L173-L180)

**No built-in natural-spawn route was found in the reviewed source.** The bundled biome/structure spawn entries and placement registrations do not wire in Giant Squids. ## Behavior and combat

- **Guardians and retaliation:** the target goals seek guardians and retaliate against living attackers, except Cachalot Whales. There is no goal that hunts players merely for being nearby. The registered health is **38 points (19 hearts)**, and its tick restores **2 health points every 100 ticks**. [Targets](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L190-L207) · [Retaliation exclusions](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/ai/goal/target/HurtByTargetGoal.java#L27-L47) · [Health](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L149-L155) · [Healing](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L228-L232)
- **Grabbing:** while in water with a living target, it begins grabbing within 7 blocks when it has line of sight, then pulls the target toward itself. Once held for more than 30 ticks, the damage branch runs every 20 ticks for **3–7 damage points** before mitigation. The separate 8-point attack attribute is not the amount used by this grab. [Melee goal](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L777-L805) · [Pull and damage](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L263-L285) · [Server damage adapter](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/Entity.java#L1772-L1776)
- **Fish targeting has an integration limit:** its target tag contains Blobfish, Cod, Salmon, Tropical Fish, Catfish, and Flying Fish, but that target-selection goal only starts when the squid is **outside water** and not captured. Its melee goal requires water. Do not assume it routinely hunts these fish underwater. [Fish tag](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/resources/data/minecraft/tags/entity_type/giant_squid_targets.json#L1-L12) · [Target condition](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L202-L205) · [Melee condition](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L777-L782)
- **Whales:** in water it looks for nearby Cachalot Whales and swims away. Giant Squids are in the whale's prey tag, and the whale's charge can damage them. The special whale capture-and-escape sequence is disconnected in this snapshot. [Avoidance](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L664-L706) · [Whale prey](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/resources/data/minecraft/tags/entity_type/cachalot_whale_targets.json#L1-L10) · [Charge and disabled capture](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityCachalotWhale.java#L684-L693)
- **Ink:** after a successful damage call, remembering a living attacker, being uncaptured, and passing a random check can trigger an ink burst. This produces particles and a sound, not an Ink Sac item. [Damage and ink](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L641-L660)

## Keeping a Giant Squid

Keep it in water with space to move. Its swimming goal favors the lower portion of sufficiently deep water, but its fit-check helper always returns true, so this is not a guarantee that it can navigate every pool. Its inherited air handler restores 300 air while in water; outside water it drains air and deals 2 drowning damage when the air counter reaches −20. It also has a nearby-water search goal and occasionally flops on land. [Swimming](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L714-L769) · [Fit helper](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L614-L616) · [Air handling](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/animal/WaterAnimal.java#L39-L58) · [Drowning threshold](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java#L491-L493) · [Water-search goal](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L190-L194) · [Land movement](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L341-L349)

There is no implemented feeding, taming, breeding, bucket capture, or player-controlled riding interaction. Its interaction method delegates to the default mob response, and it is a `WaterAnimal`, not an ageable breeding animal. Using its spawn egg directly on an existing squid cannot produce a baby. Water animals cannot be leashed, and this squid explicitly refuses portals. ## Appearance and multipart limits

The normal and blue textures are implemented, and the `Blue` flag is saved and loaded. The reviewed spawn method does not randomly select blue, so no natural rarity or breeding route is established. A separate pale overlay responds to the blocks above the squid: its pressure calculation counts water and solid blocks, so roofs can change the result. [Blue save data](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L427-L435) · [Spawn initialization](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L124-L138) · [Texture and overlay](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/client/render/RenderGiantSquid.java#L75-L98) · [Pressure calculation](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/alexsmobs/entity/EntityGiantSquid.java#L599-L611)

Its body and tentacles have ten internal parts which are positioned each tick and used for explicit nearby-entity pushes. However, those parts are not connected to the engine's normal multipart tracking and hit lookup, which handle dragon parts. Do not expect every visible tentacle to be independently hittable. The squid's larger mantle-collision method also does not replace the engine's private movement-collision method, and its old no-argument collision callback does not match the current callback. These are source integration limits, not in-game test results. ## Drops and rewards

**No ordinary item drop is supplied by the reviewed bundled data.** The default entity loot key is `minecraft:entities/giant_squid`, but no corresponding bundled table was found; a missing table resolves to an empty table. Do not assume normal Squid Ink Sac drops, Looting bonuses, or cooked drops apply. The inherited base experience reward is **1–3 XP**, subject to the ordinary player-credit and mob-loot checks. [Default loot naming](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/EntityType.java#L2064-L2066) · [Missing-table fallback](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/server/ReloadableServerRegistries.java#L115-L120) · [Experience range](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/animal/WaterAnimal.java#L34-L37) · [Experience conditions](https://github.com/HungLo2020/MattMC/blob/3e85592c4c78ebb420302360667a6c230dc0318d/src/main/java/net/minecraft/world/entity/LivingEntity.java#L1480-L1487)

A [Lost Tentacle](../items/LostTentacle.md) item is registered, but the squid's item-release code only lives inside `tickCaptured`. Its intended caller in the whale is commented out, as is the whale's Giant Squid capture trigger. Therefore this is **not a verified Survival source of Lost Tentacles**, and killing a squid is not an implemented substitute.
