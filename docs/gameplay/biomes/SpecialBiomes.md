# Special biome selection

**The Void** belongs to a dedicated flat-world setup. **Primordial Ocean** needs a distinction between the literal Normal preset and the loaded Primordial Caves dimension definition. Neither is one of the [nine ordinary Overworld ocean selections](Oceans.md). Use this guide to understand the available setup and source limits before planning a resource trip. [Normal preset][normal] · [Void flat preset][void-preset] · [Bundled Primordial dimension][primordial-dimension]

## The Void {#the-void}

**`minecraft:the_void`** is a technical biome with **no carvers and empty spawn tables**. Its only biome feature is `void_start_platform`; the biome definition itself does not supply ordinary terrain, ores, trees or food sources. This is a purpose-built blank setting, not another region to discover while exploring the Normal Overworld. [Biome definition][void] · [Normal selection][normal] [selector]

The bundled **The Void flat preset** chooses this biome, sets one layer of Air, enables features, disables lakes and supplies an empty structure override list. It is included in the visible flat-preset tag, which the flat-preset screen reads. The flat generator uses the selected biome directly; its adjustment code deliberately allows The Void's decoration in an all-air setup when features are enabled. [Preset][void-preset] · [Visible list][flat-visible] · [Preset screen][flat-screen] · [Fixed-biome generator][flat-generator] · [Decoration gate][flat-decoration]

The platform chain is connected: the biome points to a placed feature with a biome filter, that points to the configured `void_start_platform` feature, and the registered implementation builds a **33 × 33 Stone platform with one Cobblestone center** around X=8, Z=8. It places the platform three blocks above the feature origin. This is a small starting platform, not a terrain-generation or Survival-supply system. Changing the preset's feature toggle or generator changes whether this route runs. [Placed feature][void-placed] · [Configuration][void-config] · [Feature registration][features] · [Platform implementation][platform] · [Decoration caller][decorate]

The empty structure override list causes this flat setup to use no structure sets. Empty biome spawn lists likewise provide no ordinary natural candidates; they do not prevent commands, spawn eggs, spawners or entities introduced by another system. These statements describe the checked preset, not every custom world that uses the same biome ID. [Flat structure selection][flat-structures] · [Biome tables][void]

## Primordial Ocean {#primordial-ocean}

**`minecraft:primordial_ocean`** is bundled biome data associated with [Primordial Caves](../dimensions/PrimordialCaves.md), not an extra warm/cold variant of the Overworld ocean selector. Its final availability depends on **which dimension definition is used**, so reading the Normal preset JSON alone gives an incomplete answer. [Biome data][primordial] · [Normal preset][normal] · [Overworld selector][selector]

There are three separate checked source paths:

| Source path | Biome selection relevant here |
| --- | --- |
| Literal Normal preset's Primordial Caves entry | Dry Midlands and Primordial Plains; no Primordial Ocean entry |
| Bundled `dimension/primordial_caves.json` | Includes Primordial Ocean, Primordial Plains and Dry Midlands |
| Server's missing-dimension fallback | Uses the Primordial Plains-only parameter preset |

The world loader reads the dimension registry from enabled server-pack resources. During both fresh creation and saved-world loading, the final bake **prefers a matching loaded dimension entry over the selected or saved preset entry**. The client Normal creation, dedicated-server creation, and saved-world callers all pass that loaded registry. With the bundled dimension resource loaded unchanged, the effective biome source includes Primordial Ocean candidates; the literal Normal selector's omission is **not proof of its absence from the final world**. Data-pack overrides can change the definition, and existing chunks are not regenerated by this selection step. [Dimension resource][primordial-dimension] · [Loaded registry][load] · [Dimension registry codec][dimension-registry] · [Fresh client load][fresh-client] · [Creation context][creation-context] · [Fresh client bake][client-bake] · [Dedicated fresh/saved callers][server-call] · [Dedicated creation bake][server-bake] · [Saved client caller][saved-client] · [Saved-data bake][saved-bake] · [Registry precedence][bake] · [Fallback selector][fallback-preset]

This verifies selection wiring, not a specific seed, measured biome frequency, portal landing or travel itinerary. Use the dimension guide for its separate access and environment rules rather than treating this page as a newly verified Ocean travel route.

### Resources and terrain conditions

The biome's feature list includes ore-bearing geodes for Coal, Copper, Diamond, Emerald, Gold, Iron, Lapis and Redstone; separate ore-vein features; dungeons; water springs; Basalt features; Bubble Coral, Seagrass and Sea Pickle placements. The shared Primordial noise settings contain biome-specific Basalt and Obsidian surface branches. These are connected definitions, not guaranteed visible deposits or ocean-floor composition everywhere. [Biome entries][primordial] · [Shared surface rules][primordial-noise] · [Emerald geode example][emerald-geode]

The custom Bubble Coral and Seagrass patches sample heights from the generator bottom through **Y=-8**, check water and require the underlying `surface/ocean` tag, which contains Gravel, Prismarine, Deepslate and Obsidian. Their remaining support and placement checks still apply. These are distinct from Warm Ocean's coral-tree/claw/mushroom route. The Sea Pickle configuration requests a four-pickle waterlogged block and has its own placement checks. Use [Coral](../blocks/Coral.md) and [Seagrass](../blocks/Seagrass.md) for recovery and keeping harvested plants alive. [Coral placement][coral-placement] · [Coral configuration][coral-config] · [Seagrass placement][grass-placement] · [Seagrass configuration][grass-config] · [Substrate tag][ocean-ground] · [Pickle placement][pickle-placement] · [Pickle configuration][pickle-config]

The ordinary custom ore configurations use the current stone/deepslate replacement tags, including the Emerald example. The separate `ore/magma` and `ore/gravel` configurations still name **`base_stone_dwarfhollow`**, for which no bundled block-tag file was found in this snapshot. Do not treat those two vein entries as a demonstrated generation supply. Geodes and other Gravel features use different configurations; the limitation does not establish that all ores, Gravel or Magma are absent. See [Dry Midlands](DryMidlands.md#mining-geodes-versus-ordinary-veins) and [Ore resources](../blocks/OreResources.md) for the shared distinction. [Emerald vein targets][emerald-ore] · [Magma target][magma] · [Gravel target][gravel] · [Tag-match predicate][tag-match]

### Listed creatures and practical limits

The biome lists **Cod, Squid and Dolphins**, underground Glow Squid, Bats and ordinary hostile candidates, plus Drowned. It has no Axolotl, Tropical Fish or custom prehistoric-mob entry. These are candidate tables, not verified encounters. [Exact spawn lists][primordial]

Cod, Squid and Dolphin bindings use the near-sea-level water predicates. Under the shared Primordial generator's sea level **-6**, their inclusive height band is **Y=-19 through Y=-6**, with the water checks still required. Glow Squid's separate predicate requires darkness, Water and **Y≤-39** at that sea level. Drowned uses its non-river depth/random checks because Primordial Ocean is absent from the river tag. Those restrictions matter more than the biome's ocean name when judging a particular pool.
