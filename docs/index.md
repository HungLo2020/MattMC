# MattMC Official Wiki

MattMC is a fork of Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.10 with integrated content and
ongoing performance and engine work. This wiki explains MattMC's implemented
recipes, interactions and limitations. Familiar vanilla or mod names do not
necessarily mean identical behavior; check an article's source date and known
limits when a detail matters.

## Find what you need

- **Look up content:** [Blocks](gameplay/blocks/Blocks.md), [Items](gameplay/items/Items.md), [Mobs](gameplay/mobs/Mobs.md), [Biomes](gameplay/biomes/Biomes.md) and [Structures](gameplay/structures/Structures.md)
- **Start or manage a world:** [Local worlds and backups](gameplay/mechanics/LocalWorlds.md), [Multiplayer and LAN](gameplay/mechanics/MultiplayerAndLAN.md), [Dedicated servers](gameplay/mechanics/DedicatedServers.md), [Graphics and packs](gameplay/mechanics/GraphicsAndPacks.md)
- **Make and improve equipment:** [Crafting](gameplay/crafting/Crafting.md), [Smelting](gameplay/smelting/Smelting.md), [Smithing](gameplay/smithing/Smithing.md), [Enchanting](gameplay/enchanting/Enchanting.md) and [Brewing](gameplay/brewing/Brewing.md)
- **Understand systems:** [Gameplay topics](gameplay/Gameplay.md), [Mechanics](gameplay/mechanics/Mechanics.md), [Redstone](gameplay/redstone/Redstone.md) and the [curated content guide](gameplay/ContentGuide.md)

Block directories include separately registered variants and link to shared
family guides where appropriate. Those links make content easier to find;
they do not certify that every interaction has been tested. Player guides and
development verification records state their own scope.
